Lukita Maxwell (born October 27, 2001) is an Indonesian-born American actress. On television, she is known for her roles in the HBO Max series Generation (2021) and the Apple TV series Shrinking (2023–present). She also appeared in the films The Young Wife (2023) and Backrooms (2026).
