Kana Ichinose

  • Actress
  • 76 titles
  • Born 20 December 1996

Kana Ichinose (市ノ瀬 加那, December 20) is a Japanese voice actress from Hokkaido. She is affiliated with Sigma Seven. She played her first main role in 2018 as Ichigo in the anime series Darling in the Franxx.

Born in
Obihiro, Hokkaido, Japan
