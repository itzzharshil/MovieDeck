Naga Chaitanya Akkineni

  • Actor

Naga Chaitanya Akkineni is an Indian film actor who works in Telugu cinema. He debuted in the 2009 film Josh, directed by newcomer Vasu Varma and produced by Dil Raju. Subsequently, in 2017, he starred in Rarandoi Veduka Chudham, which became his career's highest grosser till date.
