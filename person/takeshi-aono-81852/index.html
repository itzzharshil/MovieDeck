Takeshi Aono

  • Actor
  • 184 titles
  • Born 19 June 1936

Aono Takeshi was a Japanese voice actor and actor affiliated with Aoni Production. He is also known to gamers as the voice of the diabolical Dr. Wily in the PlayStation-era Mega Man games.
