Sangeeth Prathap

Sangeeth Prathap is an Indian actor and editor working in the Malayalam film industry. He started his career as an editor. In 2022, He made his debut in acting through Hridayam.[2] His breakthrough role was Amal Davis in Premalu. He won the Kerala State Film Award for Best Editor in 2024 for his work on the film Little Miss Rawther.
