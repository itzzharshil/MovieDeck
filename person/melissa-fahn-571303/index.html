Melissa Fahn

  • Actress
  • 11 titles
  • Born 28 April 1967

Melissa Fahn is an American voice actress and singer for anime dubs, such as Cowboy Bebop, Digimon, Eureka Seven, FLCL, Noein, Zatch Bell! and Fate/stay night. She is also known for her role as Gaz Membrane in the American animated television series Invader Zim. She starred in the Broadway performance of Wicked and various theatre projects in Los Angeles.
