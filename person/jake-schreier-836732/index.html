Jacob Stacey Schreier (born 1981) is an American director. He was a founding member of Waverly Films, a Brooklyn-based filmmaking collective. He joined Park Pictures in 2006, releasing his first feature film, Robot & Frank, in 2012. In 2015, he released Paper Towns, an adaptation of the 2008 novel by John Green. He directed the 2025 Marvel Studios superhero film Thunderbolts*.
