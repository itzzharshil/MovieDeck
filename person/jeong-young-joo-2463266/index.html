Jeong Young-joo

Jeong Young-joo (born May 23, 1971) is a South Korean actress. A graduate of the Seoul Institute of the Arts, she made her debut in 1994. Known for playing strong, dominant female characters, she has acted in television, film, and on the stage. She currently serves as a media acting lecturer at Konkuk University.
