Romi Park

  • Actress
  • 201 titles
  • Born 22 January 1972

Romi Park is a Japanese actress and voice actress of Korean descent. At the first Seiyuu Awards, she won an award for her portrayal of Nana Oosaki in Nana. She is known for voicing tough, calm, and mature preteen or teenage boys.
