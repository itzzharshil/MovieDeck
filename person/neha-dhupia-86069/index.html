Neha Dhupia

  • Actress
  • 57 titles
  • Born 27 August 1980

Neha Dhupia is an Indian actress and model who works in Hindi, Punjabi, Telugu, and Malayalam. She has won the Femina Miss India 2002 pageant and was among the top 10 finalists in Miss Universe in the same year. She has also been one of the leaders of MTV Roadies since Season 14.

Cochin, Kerala, India
