Finn Little (born June 9, 2006) is an Australian actor. He started acting at age 5, appearing in numerous regional and national television commercials. Since then he has appeared in Taylor Sheridan's series "Yellowstone" (2018) and film "Those Who Wish Me Dead" (2021).
