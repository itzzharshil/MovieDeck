Anna Rynee Bridget Lambe (born September 25, 2000) is a Canadian Inuk actress. Her debut screen role was in the sports drama film, The Grizzlies (2018), for which she received a nomination for the Canadian Screen Award for Best Supporting Actress at the 7th Canadian Screen Awards. Lambe also is known for her roles in Heart of the Beast (2026), The Social Reckoning (2026) and True Detective: Night Country.
