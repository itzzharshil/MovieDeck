C. Prem Kumar

  • Director
  • 11 titles
  • Born 20 September 1976

Prem Kumar Chandran is an Indian film cinematographer, director & screenwriter. He began his career as the DOP of Pandiraj's Pasanga (2009). Naduvula Konjam Pakkatha Kaanom (2012) starring Vijay Sethupathi was based on some comical events that happened during Prem Kumar's wedding, their acquaintance during the film's production led to them joining hands again for Prem Kumar's Directional Debut '96' (2018).
