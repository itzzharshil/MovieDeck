Dong-hee Shin, better known by his stage name Shindong (lit. meaning: "prodigy"), is a South Korean singer, Rapper, MC, and Radio personality. He is best known as a member of the K-pop boy band Super Junior and its subgroups Super Junior-T and Super Junior-H.
