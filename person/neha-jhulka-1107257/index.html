Neha Jhulka

  • Actress
  • 3 titles
  • Born 30 January 1984

Neha Jhulka is an Indian Film and TV actress. She mainly appeared in South Indian Films as well as Bollywood and Telugu Movies. she was started her acting career in 2007 with a Telugu Movie Okkadunnadu. in this same year she played a Telugu Movie Viyyala Vari Kayyalu and a Bollywood Movie Kaisay Kahein. Neha Jhulka also appeared in Two Television Serials Dill Mill Gayyee and Geet Hui Sabse Parayi.
