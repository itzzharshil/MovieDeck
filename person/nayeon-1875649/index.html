NAYEON

  • Actress
  • 58 titles
  • Born 22 September 1995

Im Na-yeon, known mononymously as Nayeon, is a South Korean singer under JYP Entertainment. She is a member of the girl group TWICE, formed through the reality television show Sixteen (2015).

Nayeon released her eponymous debut extended play, Im Nayeon, in June 2022. It peaked at number 1 on South Korea's Circle Album Chart and number 7 on the US Billboard 200, making her the first South Korean soloist in history to enter the latter chart's top 10. The lead single, Pop!, reached number 2 on the Circle Digital Chart in South Korea and the top 10 in six countries. In 2024, Nayeon earned her second number-one entry on the Circle Album Chart and a top-ten entry on the US Billboard 200 with her second extended play Na.
