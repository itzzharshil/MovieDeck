Jodie Resther

Jodie Lynn Resther (born November 28, 1978) is a Canadian actress and singer. She is best known for her television roles including Kiki in Are You Afraid of the Dark? (1992 - 1996) and the voice of Francine Frensky in Arthur (1996 - 2022).
