Anandhi

Anandhi (born December 9, 1992) is an Indian actress who works primarily in Tamil and Telugu cinema.

She made her acting debut in Telugu cinema with Bus Stop (2012), followed by Priyathama Neevachata Kusalama (2013) and Green Signal (2014). In 2014, she made her Tamil film debut with Poriyaalan and gained wider recognition for playing the title role in Kayal. She subsequently starred in Tamil productions including Chandi Veeran (2015), Trisha Illana Nayanthara (2015), Visaranai (2016), Enakku Innoru Per Irukku (2016), Kadavul Irukaan Kumaru (2016), Pandigai (2017), Rubaai (2017), En Aaloda Seruppa Kaanom (2017), Mannar Vagaiyara (2018), and Pariyerum Perumal (2018).

Her later Tamil releases include Irandam Ulagaporin Kadaisi Gundu (2019), Kamali from Nadukkaveri (2021), Nadhi (2022), Raavana Kottam (2023), Mangai (2024), and White Rose (2024). In Telugu cinema, she appeared in Zombie Reddy (2021), Sridevi Soda Center (2021), and Itlu Maredumilli Prajaneekam (2022), along with the bilingual thriller Adrishyam / Yugi (2022) and Custody (2023).

On streaming platforms, she appeared in the series Live Telecast (2021) and Arabia Kadali (2025).
