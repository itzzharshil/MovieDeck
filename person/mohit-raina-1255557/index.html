Mohit Raina

Mohit Raina is an Indian actor known for his work in television and films. Born on August 14, 1982, in Jammu, India, he gained widespread recognition for portraying Lord Shiva in the hit TV series Devon Ke Dev...Mahadev (2011-2014). His powerful performance and screen presence made him a household name. Raina later ventured into films, making his Bollywood debut in Uri: The Surgical Strike (2019). He has also worked in web series like Kaafir (2019) and Mumbai Diaries 26/11 (2021), showcasing his versatility as an actor.
