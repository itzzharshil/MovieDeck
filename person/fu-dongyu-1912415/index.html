Fu Dongyu

Fu Dongyu, a famous director and screenwriter in the Mainland, graduated from the Film and Television Director Department of Shanghai Theater Academy. His works have successively won domestic and foreign awards such as Huabiao Award, Feitian Award, Golden Rooster Award, Five One Project Award, and Magnolia Award.
