Issac Ryan Brown

  • Actor
  • 32 titles
  • Born 12 July 2005

Issac Ryan Brown is an American actor, rapper and singer. He is known for his role as Booker in Disney Channel's Raven's Home, which earned him a nomination at the 48th Daytime Emmy Awards for Outstanding Principal Performance in a Children's Program, and his role as Augustus "Gus" Porter in The Owl House.
