About Crayon Shinchan the Movie: Spooky! My Yokai Vacation

One summer, strange phenomena are reported across Japan. The Nohara family travels to Hiroshi's hometown in Akita for a visit. While preparing for the trip, a mysterious shadow secretly watches Shin-chan playing monster pretend games. After arriving, Shin-chan finds flyers for "Yokai Village" with "Chocobi All-You-Can-Eat!" and begs the family to go. On the way, they wander deep into remote mountains and accidentally stumble into a strange space — the entrance to the "Land of Yokai," a realm forbidden to humans.Can Shin-chan and his family survive their biggest crisis ever and make it back to the human world?
