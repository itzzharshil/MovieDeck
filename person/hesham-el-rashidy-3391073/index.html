Hesham El-Rashidy

  • Director
  • 4 titles

Hesham El-Rashidy is an Egyptian filmmaker and visionary director known for his innovative approach to storytelling, visual effects, and cutting-edge cinematic techniques.

He studied at the International Academy for Media Science, where his short films received recognition at local and international film festivals. In 2013, he earned a Filmmaking Certificate from the New York Film Academy and later received an editing certificate from Apple.

El-Rashidy is the founder of TREND VFX, a post-production company specializing in creative visual storytelling and advanced visual effects.

After directing numerous local and international commercials, he made his feature film debut with MAKO (ماكو) (2021), a shark thriller released internationally. He later directed Hadas Bel Feel (حدث بالفعل) (2023) and the Arabic adaptation of Good Girls (2024).
