Phoebe Bridgers (born Phoebe Lucille Bridgers, 17 August 1994, Pasadena, California) is an American singer-songwriter, record producer, and actress. She began writing songs at 11 and attended the Los Angeles County High School for the Arts, where she studied vocal performance. After connecting with Ryan Adams at age 20, she released her debut single "Killer" in 2015 on his PAX AM label. She signed with Dead Oceans and released her debut album Stranger in the Alps (2017), followed by Punisher (2020) and Lost Weekend (14 August 2026, her first solo album in six years, exploring themes including the recent death of her father). She is also a member of the supergroup boygenius (with Julien Baker and Lucy Dacus) and the project Better Oblivion Community Center. She has won four Grammy Awards from eleven nominations, including Best Alternative Music Album for The Record (2023) and awards for her collaboration with SZA. Her discography and filmography include: Stranger in the Alps (2017); Better Oblivion Community Center (2019); Punisher (2020); The Record (2023, boygenius); Lost Weekend (2026); On Video (2026, from the Primetime soundtrack). On screen: Between Two Ferns: The Movie (2019, cameo); I Saw the TV Glow (2024, dir. Jane Schoenbrun) (Justice Smith, Jasper Newell); Primetime (2026, dir. Lance Oppenheim) (Robert Pattinson, Merritt Wever, Skyler Gisondo, Matthew Maher) — her first substantial acting role, as Del Harvey, a decoy on To Catch a Predator. She also lent her voice to Invention 13 (2026, voice).
