Masatomo Nakazawa (中澤 まさとも, Nakazawa Masatomo, February 14, 1983) is a Japanese voice actor and actor from Nerima, Tokyo. He is attached to Tokyo Actor's Consumer's Cooperative Society (Haikyō). His real name and former stage name is Masatomo Nakazawa (中澤 匡智, Nakazawa Masatomo). His nickname is Namo (なも).
