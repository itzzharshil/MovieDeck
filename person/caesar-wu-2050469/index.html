Caesar Wu

  • 17 titles
  • Born 19 October 1996

Caesar Wu is a Chinese actor, born in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China.

His acting debut came in 2018, when he appeared in ‘Meteor Garden' with Dylan Wang, Darren Chen, and Connor Leong, who soon became known as ‘The F4’. Later on, in 2018, he participated in the recording of the Hunan TV variety show ‘Happy Camp’ and the CCTV sports channel football theme talk show ‘Qi Tan Eleven’.
