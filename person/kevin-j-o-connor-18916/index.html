Kevin J. O'Connor (born November 15, 1963) is N American actor. He is known for playing character roles in films such as There Will Be Blood, The Mummy, Lord of Illusions, F/X2, and Van Helsing. He is a favorite of writer/director Stephen Sommers, who usually casts him in his films.
