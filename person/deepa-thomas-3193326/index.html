Deepa Thomas

  • Actress
  • 10 titles
  • Born 21 November 1996

Deepa Thomas (born November 22, 1996) is an Indian actress who works predominantly in Malayalam cinema. She made her feature film debut with a minor role in the medical thriller *Virus* (2019).

She gained wider recognition for her supporting role in the family drama *#Home* (2021). Her subsequent Malayalam projects include the romantic comedy *Sulaikha Manzil* (2023), the comedy drama *Njaan Ippo Entha Cheyya* (2023), and the satirical drama *Perumani* (2024). She also expanded into Telugu cinema with the period drama *Rao Bahadur* (2026).
