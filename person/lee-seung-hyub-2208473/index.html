Lee Seung-hyub, also known as J.Don, is a South Korean rapper, singer, instrumentalist, songwriter, and actor. Lee officially debuted in 2013 and rose to prominence as the leader of the South Korean band N.Flying. In 2016, he debuted as an actor in the series "Entertainer".
