Saurav Gurjar (born 26 September 1985) is an Indian professional wrestler and actor. He is currently signed to WWE, where he performs on the Raw brand under the ring name Sanga, as a member of Indus Sher, alongside Veer Mahaan and Jinder Mahal. He is best known for his role as Bhima in the TV show Mahabharat. He is a former National Kickboxing Gold Medalist.
