Mikey Madison (born Mikaela Madison Rosberg, 25 March 1999, Los Angeles, California) is an American actress. A former competitive horseback rider, she was homeschooled after the seventh grade and began acting at 14. She rose to prominence as Max Fox in the FX series Better Things (2016–2022), then appeared in Once Upon a Time in Hollywood (2019) and Scream (2022). Her breakthrough as Anora in Anora (2024) earned her the Academy Award for Best Actress (2025) and the BAFTA Award for Best Actress, making her the first Gen Z winner in an acting category at the Oscars. Her filmography includes: Liza, Liza, Skies Are Grey (2017); Monster (2018) (Lili Taylor, Gina Gershon); Once Upon a Time in Hollywood (2019) (Leonardo DiCaprio, Brad Pitt); Scream (2022) (Melissa Barrera, Jenna C. Olson); All Souls (2023) (Jenna Ortega, Liza Soberano); Anora (2024) (Mark Eydelshteyn, Aleksei Serebryakov); The Social Reckoning (2026). TV: Better Things (2016–2022) (Pamela Adlon, Jen Kain), Lady in the Lake (2024) (Ruth Negga, Michael C. Hall).
