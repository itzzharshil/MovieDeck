Mika Abdalla

  • Actress
  • 12 titles
  • Born 13 May 2000

Mikayla Sofia Abdalla (born May 13, 2000) is a first-generation American actress of Bulgarian, Lebanese, Greek, and German descent. Some of her early acting work includes Barney & Friends, where she made her acting debut in 2008, and An American Girl: Saige Paints the Sky (2013).

Abdalla is best known for starring as McKeyla McAlister in the Netflix original series Project Mc² (2015-2017), earning herself a Young Artist Award nomination in 2016. She starred in notable projects such as Sex Appeal (2022) and Snack Shack (2024) before starring in her breakout role on Off Campus (2026-present) as Allie Hayes.
