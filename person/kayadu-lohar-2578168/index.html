Kayadu Lohar

  • 13 titles
  • Born 11 April 2000

Kayadu Lohar (born April 11, 2000) is an Indian actress who works across Kannada, Malayalam, Telugu, and Tamil cinema.

She made her feature film debut in the Kannada film Mugilpete (2021). In 2022, she portrayed Nangeli in the Malayalam period film Pathonpatham Noottandu (2022) and made her Telugu debut with Alluri (2022). She subsequently made her Tamil debut with Dragon (2025) and appeared in the Malayalam film Oru Jaathi Jaathakam (2025).

In 2026, Lohar appeared in the Telugu film Funky (2026), the Malayalam productions Pallichattambi (2026) and I'm Game (2026), and the Tamil features Idhayam Murali (2026) and Immortal (2026), followed by the Telugu action drama The Paradise (2026).
