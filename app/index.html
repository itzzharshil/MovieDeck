Questions

Why isn't it on the Google Play Store?

MovieDeck is shared directly as an APK file. It's signed, so Android will only install updates that come from us.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. On iPhone, open moviedeck.xyz in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

How do I update it?

Most updates appear on their own, because the app loads the live site. When there's a new version of the app itself, download it from this page and install it over the old one. Your watchlist stays.
