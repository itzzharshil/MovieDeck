Gara Takashima

  • Actress
  • 67 titles
  • Born 2 March 1954

Gara Takashima is a veteran Japanese voice actress, she's been affiliated with Haikyou since 2010. She's been affiliated with them before until April 2008, after which she joined Across Entertainment until October 2009, when she went freelance until re-joining Haikyou.

She is married to fellow voice actor Banjou Ginga.

Tokyo, Japan
