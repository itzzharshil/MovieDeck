Rachel Hilson

Rachel Naomi Hilson (born 1995) is an American actress. She started her career in 2010 with a recurring role on the CBS legal drama The Good Wife as Nisa Dalmar. In 2025, she co-led the Max crime thriller series Duster as FBI agent Nina Hayes. She has also had recurring roles on NBC's Rise and This Is Us, and a lead role on the Hulu teen drama Love, Victor.
