Shilpa Shinde

  • Actress
  • 8 titles
  • Born 28 August 1977

Shilpa Shinde is an Indian television actress. Shilpa is currently working in Do Dil Ek Jaan,after replacing Kamya Panjabi. Shilpa had also acted in a DD1 serial Miss India, Hatim on Star Plus and as Chitra in the prime time serial on Star Plus, Sanjeevani - A Medical Boon. Shilpa has acted in two Telugu films-Deshari Narain Rao's "Chhina" and Suresh Verma's "Shivani".
