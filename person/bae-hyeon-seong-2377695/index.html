Bae Hyeon-seong

  • Actor
  • 14 titles
  • Born 3 May 1999

A rising South Korean actor hailing from the Bunseong Bae clan and a graduate of Hanseong High School. He is currently signed with Awesome ENT. He officially made his debut in 2018 through the series What's Wrong with Secretary Kim. In 2021, he rose to prominence playing the role of Jang Hong-do in Hospital Playlist Season 2. In 2022, he gradually built his recognition by appearing in series such as Our Blues and Gaus Electronics. In 2024, he starred as Kang Hae-jun in Family by Choice, a remake of the popular Chinese drama Go Ahead, displaying fresh and natural acting skills. In 2025, he starred in Shin's Project, which recorded a peak viewership rating of 9.1% (with a real-time peak of 10.5%), ranking first in its time slot across cable channels.
