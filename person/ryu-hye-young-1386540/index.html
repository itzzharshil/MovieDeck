Ryu Hye-young

  • Actress
  • 29 titles

Ryu Hye-young (born March 28, 1991) is a South Korean actress and model. She is known for her role as Sung Bo-ra in the hit drama series Reply 1988 (2015-2016), Shim Eun-Joo in Dear My Room (2018) and as Kang Sol A in the recent drama series Law School (2021).
