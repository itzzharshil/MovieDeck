Nanae Takahashi

  • Actress
  • 74 titles
  • Born 23 December 1978

Nanae Takahashi is a retired Japanese professional wrestler and the founder of SEAdLINNNG, as well as a co-founder of World Wonder Ring Stardom. She was most recently signed to Dream Star Fighting Marigold, where she held her final match at Marigold Shine Forever. She has wrestled for prominent Japanese promotions All Japan Women's Pro-Wrestling and Pro Wrestling Sun, and is a two-time women's world champion in major professional wrestling promotions.

Born in
Kawaguchi, Saitama, Japan
