Olivia Morris

Olivia Morris (born January 29, 1997) is an English film actress. She is from Kingston-upon-Thames, Greater London. Morris joined the National Youth Theatre and went on to train at the Royal Welsh College of Music and Drama, graduating in 2018. She is known for her role as Jenny in the Indian-Telugu action-drama film, RRR (2022).
