Marie Kreutzer

  • Director
  • 14 titles
  • Born 25 August 1977

Marie Kreutzer is an Austrian filmmaker. She studied at the Vienna Film Academy with Walter Wippersberg. Her films have been screened at the Berlin International Film Festival, 70th San Sebastián International Film Festival, 2022 Cannes Film Festival, 2022 Toronto International Film Festival, and 2022 New York Film Festival.
