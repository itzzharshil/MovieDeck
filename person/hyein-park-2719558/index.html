Hyein Park (born August 25, 1997) is a Korean-Canadian storyboard artist and voice actress. She began her career working on Peg + Cat before moving on to larger projects, such as Dragons: Race to the Edge and the animated films Max Steel: Team Turbo and Max Steel Team Turbo: Fusion Tek.
