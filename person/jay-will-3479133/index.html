**Jay Will** is an American actor. Celebrated for his charisma, dynamic energy, and compelling emotional range, he gained widespread breakout recognition for his starring role as Tyson Mitchell in Taylor Sheridan's acclaimed Paramount+ crime drama series *Tulsa King*.

Born and raised in Queens, New York, Will discovered a passion for the performing arts during his youth. He pursued formal acting training at the Juilliard School, graduating as a member of Group 48 in 2019. His classical training provided him with a strong foundation in stagecraft, vocal technique, and text analysis, which he quickly translated into on-screen opportunities.

After early appearances in short films and guest television roles on series such as *The Marvelous Mrs. Maisel* and *Evil*, Will landed his career-defining breakthrough in 2022 when he was cast alongside Sylvester Stallone in *Tulsa King*. As Tyson Mitchell, a quick-witted, ambitious cab driver who becomes Dwight "The General" Manfredi’s trusted personal chauffeur and right-hand man, his performance became an emotional anchor and fan-favorite dynamic in the series.

Beyond *Tulsa King*, Will headlined the acclaimed biographical drama film *Rob Peace* (2024), directed by Chiwetel Ejiofor. Portraying the real-life title figure, a brilliant young man caught between the academic promise of Yale University and the realities of his Newark neighborhood, Will delivered a powerhouse dramatic performance that drew widespread praise from critics and festival audiences. Combining classical training with compelling screen charisma, he stands as one of the most promising young actors working in contemporary American cinema and television.
