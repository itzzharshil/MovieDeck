Bhagyashri Borse

  • 11 titles

Bhagyashri Borse (born 6 May 1999) is an Indian actress and model who works predominantly in Telugu and Tamil cinema, as well as in Hindi films. Before beginning her acting career, she worked as a model and appeared in commercial campaigns.

She appeared in the Hindi films *Yaariyan 2* (2023), as Raajlaxmi, and Kabir Khan's biographical sports drama *Chandu Champion* (2024). Borse made her Telugu debut opposite Ravi Teja in Harish Shankar's romantic action drama *Mr. Bachchan* (2024), for which she won the SIIMA Award for Best Female Debut – Telugu. She later appeared in Gowtam Tinnanuri's action thriller *Kingdom* (2025), alongside Vijay Deverakonda, and in *Andhra King Taluka* (2025), opposite Ram Pothineni.

Borse made her Tamil debut opposite Dulquer Salmaan in Selvamani Selvaraj's period drama *Kaantha* (2025), for which she won the Ananda Vikatan Cinema Award for Best Debut Actress – Tamil. Her other credits include *Lenin* (2026), alongside Akhil Akkineni, the Tamil drama *Seyon* (2026), and the music video single "Radhimaa" (2026), composed by Sai Abhyankkar.
