About Chainsaw Man - The Movie: Reze Arc

In a world where devils are born from humanity’s fears, Denji has only ever dreamed of living a simple life, eating good food, finding happiness, and experiencing the warmth of being loved. Then, one rainy afternoon, fate leads him to Reze, a mysterious girl working at a small café. With her disarming smile, playful charm, and unexpected kindness, she offers Denji something he has rarely known—a glimpse of an ordinary life beyond bloodshed and chaos.
