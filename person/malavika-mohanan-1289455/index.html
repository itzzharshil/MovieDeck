Malavika Mohanan

  • Actress
  • 17 titles

Malavika Mohanan (born August 4, 1993) is an Indian actress who works predominantly in Malayalam, Tamil, and Hindi cinema. She made her acting debut in *Pattam Pole* (2013), followed by *Nirnayakam* (2015) and her Kannada debut *Naanu Mattu Varalakshmi* (2016). She gained wider recognition for her performance in the drama *Beyond the Clouds* (2017) and appeared in *The Great Father* (2017).

Mohanan made her Tamil cinema debut in *Petta* (2019), followed by leading roles in *Master* (2021) and *Maaran* (2022). Her subsequent releases include the romantic drama *Christy* (2023), the historical action feature *Thangalaan* (2024), and the action film *Yudhra* (2024). She later appeared in *Hridayapoorvam* (2025) and made her Telugu cinema debut in *The Raja Saab* (2026).
