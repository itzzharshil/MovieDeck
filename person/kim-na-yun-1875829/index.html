Kim Na-yun

Kim Na Yun, born in Seoul, is a member of the South Korean girl group Momoland. In 2016, she became a contestant on Mnet's reality survival show, Finding Momoland. She finished the competition in third place, thus becoming a member of the girl group MOMOLAND.
