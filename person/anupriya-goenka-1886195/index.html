Anupriya Goenka

  • Actress

Anupriya Goenka is an Indian actress and model who appears in Hindi and Telugu films. She first came to fame in 2013 as the face of UPA government's Bharat Nirman ad campaign and for playing a lesbian in India's first ever lesbian ad for the brand Myntra.

Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh, India
