Takayuki Kondo (近藤 孝行, Kondō Takayuki, born June 5, 1978) is a Japanese voice actor born in Tottori Prefecture. He is affiliated with Aoni Production. He was formerly affiliated with the Tokyo Actor's Consumer's Cooperative Society (Haikyo), Air Agency and Black Ship. He belongs to Aoni Production since April 1, 2023.
