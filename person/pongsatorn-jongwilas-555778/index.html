Pongsatorn Jongwilas

  • Actor
  • 33 titles
  • Born 4 October 1982

Phuak Pongsatorn Jongwilas is a Thai actor and DJ. He went to Kasetsart University in Bangkok where he graduated with a major in Journalism. He's been a close friend of Bo Thanakorn Chinakul since elementary school. Presently, Phuak is a DJ at 94 EFM and the owner of LOLLIPOP®, a kids' fashion line.
