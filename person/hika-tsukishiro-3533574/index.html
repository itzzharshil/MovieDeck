Hika Tsukishiro (月城 日花, Tsukishiro Hika, born March 17, 2004) is a Japanese voice actress born in Fukuoka Prefecture. Her former stage name is Hika Kugisaki (釘崎 日花, Kugisaki Hika). She is affiliated with Haikyo.

She graduated from the Yoyogi Animation Academy in 2021 under the name Hika Kugisaki. After graduating, she joined Haikyo and changed her stage name to Hika Tsukishiro.
