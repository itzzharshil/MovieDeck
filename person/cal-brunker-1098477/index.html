Cal Brunker

  • Writer
  • 20 titles
  • Born 16 November 1978

Cal Brunker is a Canadian filmmaker, animator, and screenwriter. He is best known for directing Spin Master Entertainment's hit animated feature franchise, including "PAW Patrol: The Movie" (2021), "PAW Patrol: The Mighty Movie" (2023), and "PAW Patrol: The Dino Movie" (2026). His directorial credits also include animated films such as "Escape from Planet Earth" (2013) and "The Nut Job 2: Nutty by Nature" (2017).
