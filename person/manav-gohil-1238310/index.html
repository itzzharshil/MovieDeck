Manav Gohil (born 9 November 1974) is an Indian actor. He has been active in the Hindi film industry for more than a decade and is best known for his roles in Hindi television shows, including the soap opera Kahaani Ghar Ghar Kii, the dance reality show Nach Baliye 2, the crime drama C.I.D., the drama Shaadi Mubarak and Tenali Rama. He is also known for Super 30 (2019), Josh (2000) and Baaghi 3 (2020).
