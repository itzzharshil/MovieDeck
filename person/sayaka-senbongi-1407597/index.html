Sayaka Senbongi (千本木 彩花, Senbongi Sayaka, born November 24, 1995) is a Japanese voice actress (seiyuu) from Saitama Prefecture. She belongs to I'm Enterprise. She is married to fellow voice actor Tasuku Hatanaka.

Saitama, Japan
