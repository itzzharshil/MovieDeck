Supriya Pilgaonkar (born 17 August 1967) , often known by her screen name Supriya, is a Hindi/Marathi actress.She has acted in a handful of television serials; the most popular of them are Tu Tu Main Main, Kuch Rang Pyar Ke Aise Bhi, Sasural Genda Phool, Radhaa Ki Betiyaan Kuch Kar Dikhayengi and Kadvee Khattee Meethi.
