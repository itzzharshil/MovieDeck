Dibyendu Bhattacharya

  • Actor
  • 59 titles
  • Born 11 November 1975

Dibyendu Bhattacharya is an Indian actor known for his work in Hindi and Bengali cinema. Born in Behala, he made his acting debut in 2001 with "Monsoon Wedding," alongside Naseeruddin Shah. He holds a Bachelor of Arts degree from Vivekananda College and is an alumnus of the National School of Drama.

Notable for performances in "Dev D.," "Chittagong," "Black Friday," and "Dhan Dhana Dhan Goal," Dibyendu's filmography includes hits like "Ab Tak Chhappan" and "Lootera." Beyond films, Dibyendu made a mark on television with "24" (2013) and featured in web series like "Sacred Games," "Mirzapur" Season 2, and "Ray" (2021). His 2022 portrayal of Prof. Mehdi Raza in "Rocket Boys" showcased his versatility.
