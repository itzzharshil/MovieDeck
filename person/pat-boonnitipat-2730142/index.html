Pat Boonnitipat (Thai: พัฒน์ บุญนิธิพัฒน์; born June 18, 1990) is a Thai television and film director. He first became known to audiences with Project S: The Series (2017) and Bad Genius: The Series (2020). He gained critical acclaim for the film, How to Make Millions Before Grandma Dies (2024), which broke box office records across Asia and became Thailand's first submission to advance to the 15-film shortlist at the 97th Academy Awards.
