About Unbearable Christmas

Out-of-work weatherman Troy Tipton has one Christmas mission: retrieve the beloved teddy bear he pawned for holiday cash. With wife Becca, mother-in-law Dawn, and the kids in tow, Troy hits the road from Ohio to Florida in his old news van. But the family road trip gets even wilder when they discover the cherished bear is actually a priceless Teddy Roosevelt antique worth millions.
