Saja Kilani

Saja Kilani (Arabic: سجىٰ كيلاني) is a Canadian-Jordanian film actress of Palestinian descent and spoken‑word poet. She gained international recognition for her leading role in the docudrama The Voice of Hind Rajab (2025), which premiered at the 2025 Venice Film Festival to widespread acclaim. Her earlier performance in Simsim earned her the Best First Time Lead Actress Award at the Amman International Film Festival.
