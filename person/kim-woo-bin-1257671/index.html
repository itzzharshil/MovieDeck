Kim Woo-bin (born July 16, 1989) is a South Korean actor and model who is considered one of the most popular Hallyu stars. Kim's breakout year came in 2013 with two hit dramas (School 2013) and (The Heirs) and one box office hit (Friend: The Great Legacy). Kim experienced a surge in popularity overseas and he was able to cement his name as one of the most popular and successful South Korean actors ever.
