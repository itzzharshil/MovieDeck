Lily-Rose Depp

  • Actress
  • 20 titles
  • Born 27 May 1999

Lily-Rose Melody Depp (born 27 May 1999) is a French-American actress. Born to actors Johnny Depp and Vanessa Paradis, she began her acting career in film with a minor role in Tusk (2014). She pursued a career as a fashion model. She appeared in the period dramas The Dancer (2016) and The King (2019), as well as the romantic comedy A Faithful Man (2018).

In 2023, Depp starred in the HBO television drama series The Idol and contributed to its soundtrack with her single "One of the Girls", which charted on the Billboard Hot 100and surpassed one billion streams on Spotify. She received praise for her starring role in the horror film Nosferatu (2024).
