John Tui (born 11 June 1975) is a New Zealand actor known for his various supporting roles in locally filmed blockbuster films, Afa Anoa'i on the 2021–22 NBC sitcom Young Rock, and Chief Tui in Disney's 2026 live-action adaptation of Moana.
