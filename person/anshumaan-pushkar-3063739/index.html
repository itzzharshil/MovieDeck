Anshumaan Pushkar (born August 17, 1993) is an Indian actor. He is best known for playing the role of Rishi Ranjan in the Hotstar original series Grahan (2021), Jamtara as Rocky on the Netflix series Jamtara (2020-present), and Gauri Bhaiya in the film 12th Fail (2023).
