Sumedh Mudgalkar is an Indian film and television actor and dancer. He began his career as a dancer, making his debut on the Marathi reality show Dance Maharashtra Dance in 2012 at the age of 15, and later emerged as one of the finalists on Dance India Dance (Season 4).He transitioned into acting with Channel V’s youth drama Dil Dosti Dance, where he portrayed Raghavendra “Raghav” Pratap Singh. Mudgalkar gained wider recognition for his portrayal of young Sushim in the historical series Chakravartin Ashoka Samrat, establishing himself in antagonist and morally complex roles.He achieved major popularity for portraying Lord Krishna in the mythological series RadhaKrishn, including various incarnations associated with the character of Narayan. Expanding his range further, he played the antagonist Darkie in the Disney+ Hotstar series Escaype Live.In addition to television, Mudgalkar has appeared in Marathi films and music videos. His work across youth drama, historical fiction, mythology, and digital formats highlights his versatility as a performer.
