Mamta Mohandas

  • Actress
  • 80 titles
  • Born 14 November 1985

Mamta Mohandas is an Indian film actress and playback singer. She has mainly acted in Malayalam films, besides few Telugu, Tamil and Kannada productions. She has won two Filmfare Awards, for Best Female Playback Singer in Telugu in 2006 and for Best Actress in Malalayam in 2010.
