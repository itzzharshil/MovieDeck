Yoshinori Nakayama (中山 祥徳, Nakayama Yoshinori, born July 2, 1996) is a Japanese voice actor born in Aichi Prefecture who is affiliated with Ancheri. After graduating from Pro Fit Voice Actor Training Center, he joined Link Plan talent agency. On April 1, 2025, he transferred to Ancheri.

His hobbies include photography, raising animals, video editing, touring, road biking, and airsoft. His special skills are drums and filleting.
