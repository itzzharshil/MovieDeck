Olivia Taylor Dudley

Olivia Taylor Dudley was born in California, USA and the daughter of Jim and Saundra Dudley. Olivia graduated from Morro Bay High School in Morro Bay, California in 2002. She focused her attention acting in various shows such as Chernobyl Diaries. Her notable movies include The Vatican Tapes and Dumbbells. Olivia is also very popular through her short online videos in 5 Second Films. Olivia is a talented American actress with confidence and promise.
