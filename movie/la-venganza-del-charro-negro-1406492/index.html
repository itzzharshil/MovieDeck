About La venganza del Charro Negro

After several years, the fearsome Charro Negro returns with a new purpose: to take revenge on Leo San Juan and collect a debt that has remained outstanding. To achieve this, he will free some of the supernatural creatures and legends that Leo has faced throughout his adventures, forcing him to reacquaint himself with his former enemies. But this time, the Black Charro has a much more personal goal: his brother, Nando.
