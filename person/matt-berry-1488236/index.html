Matthew Charles Berry (born May 2, 1974) is a British actor, comedian, voice actor, musician, and writer known for his distinctive booming, noble voice and surreal comedic style. He is best recognized for roles in The IT Crowd, Toast of London, and What We Do in the Shadows, and voices Schlub in Dan Harmon's animated series Krapopolis. He has also released several albums showcasing his musical talents.
