Shunta Sono

  • Actor
  • 25 titles
  • Born 3 May 2002

Shunta is the youngest member of the vocal dance unit M!LK under Stardust Promotion as a part of their EBiDAN branch. He joined the group on August 28, 2018.

M!LK made their first appearance on November 24, 2014. The group made their major debut under Victor Entertainment on November 24, 2021, with the release of the 1st single titled "Ribbon".
