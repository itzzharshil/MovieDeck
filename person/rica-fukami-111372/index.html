Rica Fukami (深見 梨加, Fukami Rika, born August 8, 1963) is a Japanese seiyū (voice actress) and narrator born in the city of Chichibu, Saitama Prefecture. She formerly was affiliated with Beniya 25 Ji and Vi-Vo, but currently she is freelance. Her former stage names were 深見 理佳 and 深実 りか (both pronounced Fukami Rika).
