Park Yoon-ho (Korean: 박윤호; born April 3, 2003) is a South Korean actor. He began his acting career in 2023 with Night Has Come, gaining recognition for his supporting roles in popular K-dramas like Our Unwritten Seoul (2025) and Trigger (2025).

Following his standout performances in 2025, The Korea Times highlighted him as a "rising star" in the entertainment industry.
