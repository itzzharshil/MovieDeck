Sanjeev Tyagi

  • Actor
  • 9 titles
  • Born 29 June 1971

Sanjeev Tyagi is an Indian film and Television Actor known for his role of Abhimanyu Jindal , a police inspector in Crime Patrol Series. He had done lot of television shows and movies. Some of his movies are Baby(2015), Money Devo Bhava, Hanak, Beeru, Aazam etc. and shows like Kabhi aaye na Judaai, Kahaani Ghar Ghar Kii, Karam Dharam Apna, Apna, Junoon - Aisi Nafrat Toh Kaisa Ishq, Raavan, Adaalat, Bin Bitiya Swarg Adhoora, Million Dollar Girl, Courtroom: Sachchai Hazir Ho, Crime Patrol and Crime Patrol 2.0. and WebSeries like Suranga.
