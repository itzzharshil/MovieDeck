Janhvi Kapoor

  • Born 6 March 1997

Janhvi Kapoor (born March 6, 1997) is an Indian actress who works predominantly in Hindi and Telugu cinema. She made her feature film debut in *Dhadak* (2018), winning the Zee Cine Award for Best Female Debut. She subsequently appeared in the anthology *Ghost Stories* (2020) and earned a Filmfare Award for Best Actress nomination for the biographical drama *Gunjan Saxena: The Kargil Girl* (2020).

Her subsequent releases include *Roohi* (2021), *Good Luck Jerry* (2022), *Mili* (2022), *Bawaal* (2023), *Mr. & Mrs. Mahi* (2024), and *Ulajh* (2024). That same year, she made her Telugu cinema debut in *Devara: Part 1* (2024). Her later releases includes *Sunny Sanskari Ki Tulsi Kumari* (2025), *Param Sundari* (2025), *Homebound* (2025), the Telugu feature *Peddi* (2026).
