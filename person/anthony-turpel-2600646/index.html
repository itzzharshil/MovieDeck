Anthony Turpel, born January 26, 2000, is an American actor best known for his roles as R.J. Forrester in The Bold and the Beautiful and Felix in the Hulu television series Love, Victor.

He was nominated for a Daytime Emmy in 2017 for Outstanding Young Actor in a Drama Series.
