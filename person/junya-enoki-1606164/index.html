Junya Enoki

  • Actor
  • 123 titles
  • Born 19 October 1988

Junya Enoki is a Japanese voice actor affiliated with Atomic Monkey. His hobbies are boxing and kendo. Enoki became interested in acting while in college after watching the anime Gurren Lagann. He's a cousin of Chika Anzai.

Tokyo, Japan
