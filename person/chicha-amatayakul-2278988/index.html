Chicha Amatayakul (Thai: ชิชา อมาตยกุล; born August 5, 1993), also known as Kitty, is a Thai actress and model. She gained recognition for her role as Nanno in the Netflix anthology series Girl From Nowhere (2018-present) and is also known for her role in Daughters (2020). Amatayakul has also appeared in British crime drama The Serpent (2021) as Suda.
