Ihtzi Hurtado

  • Writer
  • 6 titles

Ihtzi Hurtado is a Mexican film director, screenwriter, and producer. She gained prominence for directing and writing contemporary Mexican comedy feature films and streaming originals.

Her notable directing and writing credits include the feature films Cosas de hombres (2020) and the Amazon Original movie Locas por el cambio (2020), as well as directing episodes for various television and streaming series.
