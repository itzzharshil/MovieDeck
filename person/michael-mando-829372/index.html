Michael Mando (born Mikel Mroué, 13 July 1981, Quebec City, Quebec, Canada) is a Canadian actor of Lebanese and Ivorian descent. Raised by his father, he lived in more than 35 homes across 10 cities on four continents before his mid-20s, growing up in Accra, Abidjan, and Beirut before returning to Canada. Fluent in French, English, Arabic, and Spanish, he studied international relations at the University of Montreal before discovering the performing arts at the Dome Theatre Program at Dawson College in 2004, where he played the male lead in all five productions and graduated in 2007. He is best known for Nacho Varga in Better Call Saul (2015–2022, 33 episodes), Vaas Montenegro in the Far Cry franchise (2012, 2021), Vic Schmidt in Orphan Black (2013–2014), and Mac Gargan / Scorpion in the Marvel Cinematic Universe. He is a two-time Canadian Screen Award and Screen Actors Guild Award nominee. His filmography includes: The Good Lie (2012) (Jennifer Connelly, John Hawkes); The Colony (2013) (Jason Statham, Christina Hendricks); Elysium (2013, uncredited) (Matt Damon, Jodie Foster); Make Your Move (2013) (Shailene Woodley, Nick Jonas); Spider-Man: Homecoming (2017) (Tom Holland, Michael Keaton); The Hummingbird Project (2018) (Jason Clarke, Sienna Miller); Spider-Man: Far From Home (2019) (Tom Holland, Michael Keaton); King Ivory (2024) (Mia Goth, Jesse Plemons); Spider-Man: Brand New Day (2026) (Tom Holland, Zendaya); Batman: Knightfall (2026, voice) (Robert Pattinson, Colin Farrell). TV: Orphan Black (2013–2014) (Tatiana Maslany, Elijah Wilkinson); Better Call Saul (2015–2022) (Bob Odenkirk, Rhea Seehorn); Dope Thief (2025) (Sebastian Stan, Gillian Jacobs); Criminal (2027).
