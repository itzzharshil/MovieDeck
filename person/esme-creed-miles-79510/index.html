Esmé Creed-Miles (born 5 February 2000) is an English actress, known for starring in the Amazon Video series Hanna. Creed-Miles was born in England and attended the English boarding school Bedales. She is the daughter of actors Charlie Creed-Miles and Samantha Morton.
