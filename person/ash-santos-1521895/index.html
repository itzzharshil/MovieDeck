Ashley Marie Rockwood (born Ashley Marie Santos), known professionally as Ash Santos, is an American actress. Ash was born in the Bronx, New York. Her father is Dominican and Puerto Rican, and her mother is from the Dominican Republic. Ash was raised in the small town of Montgomery, NY. There she discovered her love for sports, enjoyed playing softball, field hockey, and competitive cheerleading. She always expressed an interest in acting, although she never pursued it growing up. She relocated to Salt Lake City, UT and was able to start her acting career in their small film market. While working as an actress in Utah she was able to add eleven films, six commercials, a series, and plenty of industrials to her resume.
