Shivangi Joshi

  • Actress
  • 9 titles

Shivangi Joshi is an Indian television actress known for playing the roles of Poonam in Begusarai and Naira in Yeh Rishta Kya Kehlata Hai. She was also seen as Ayat in the Colours daily soap Beintehaa.
