Eiko Masuyama (増山 江威子, Masuyama Eiko, April 22, 1936 – May 20, 2024) was a Japanese actress, voice actress and narrator. Her real name was Tomoko Masada (政田 知子, Masada Tomoko). She worked for Aoni Production.
