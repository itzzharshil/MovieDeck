From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Erika Ellice Sotres Starr (Mexico City, February 6, 1969), also known as Issabela Camil, is a Mexican actress. She is also the sister of Mexican actor Jaime Camil and is married to fellow actor and producer Sergio Mayer. Her parents are Armando Sotres and the American model Tony Starr.
