Hina Youmiya

  • Actress
  • 59 titles
  • Born 26 March 2000

Hina Youmiya (羊宮 妃那, Yōmiya Hina, born March 26, 2000) is a Japanese voice actress (seiyuu) from Nara Prefecture who is affiliated with Aoni Production. In 2024, she won the New Voice Actor Award at the 18th Seiyuu Awards.

Nara, Japan
