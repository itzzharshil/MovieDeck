Genki Kawamura

  • Writer
  • 42 titles
  • Born 12 March 1979

Genki Kawamura is a Japanese filmmaker, producer, and bestselling author known for blending high-concept storytelling with introspective, human themes. With Exit 8, he adapts the minimalist indie game by Kotake Create into a psychologically rich, liminal horror experience, exploring perception, guilt, and the blurred boundaries between games and cinema. His work often transforms simple premises into immersive narratives that invite audiences to confront their inner worlds
