Stephen Kalyn

Stephen Kalyn (born April 24, 1998) is a Canadian actor. The beginning of his career contained a handful of credits in multiple Canadian TV series.

Kalyn has collaborated several times with Prime Video on their streaming platform which includes Cruel Intentions (2024) & Motorheads (2025), a recurring role as Greg in Gen V (2025), and most notably one of the leading roles in Off Campus (2026-present) as Dean Di Laurentis.

He's also made his way to the big screen starring in projects like Cascade (2023) and Whistle (2025).
