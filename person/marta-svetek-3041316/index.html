Marta Svetek

  • Actress
  • 9 titles

Marta Svetek is a Slovenian actress known for Valorant (2020), Five Nights at Freddy's: Security Breach (2021), Battlefield 2042 (2021) and action-thriller I Am Rage (2023). Based in London, United Kingdom, she has lent her voice and motion capture performances to several high profile video games, as well as appearing in independent feature films, achieving particular success in the action/thriller and sci-fi genres, in part due to her advanced combat and weapons training. She has been recognized by critics for her ability to portray a compelling antagonist and anti-hero. In addition to being involved in UK-US co-productions, as well as a number of North American, European and Asian video games, she is now working on expanding her screen work, particularly in the fantasy, sci-fi, action and thriller genres.
