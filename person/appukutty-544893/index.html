Appukutty

  • Actor
  • 52 titles
  • Born 22 August 1984

Sivabalan or known as Appukutty is an Indian actor, who mainly appears in Tamil films. He became recognized following his performance in Vennila Kabadi Kuzhu. His role as Azhagarsami in Azhagarsamiyin Kudhirai was praised by film critics and fetched him the National Film Award for Best Supporting Actor.
