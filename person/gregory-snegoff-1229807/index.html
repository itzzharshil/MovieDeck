Gregory Snegoff was born on June 22, 1955 in Santa Monica, California, USA. He is an actor and writer, known for Golgo 13: The Professional (1983), Wicked City (1987) and Driving Miss Daisy (1989). He is married to Fiorella Capuano. He was previously married to Lisa Michelson.
