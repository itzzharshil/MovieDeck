Lukman Avaran

  • 45 titles

Lukman Avaran (born 22 May 1990) is an Indian actor who primarily works in Malayalam cinema. Dayom Panthrandum (2013) was the first theatre-released movie in his career. He acted the lead role in the 2021 movie Operation Java directed by Tharun Moorthy. He was mainly noted for his performances in the movies Operation Java, Shibu, Virus, Thallumaala, Saudi Vellakka.
