Max Bernstein is a Los Angeles-based guitarist and musical director. He has played guitar with Taylor Swift, Miley Cyrus, Demi Lovato, Kesha, Liz Phair, and more; and musical directed for artists such as Weezer, Clairo, LP, Hayley Kiyoko, Betty Who, Bryce Vine, Ingrid Andress, Dove Cameron, Fletcher, Noah Cyrus, Kesha, Bazzi, The Night Game, Emblem3, Bonnie McKee, the American Idol Tour, 98°, Krewella, and more.
