Fiona Gubelmann

  • Actress
  • 28 titles
  • Born 30 March 1980

Fiona Gubelmann is an American actress best known for her prominent television roles as Dr. Morgan Reznick on the ABC medical drama The Good Doctor and Jenna Mueller opposite Elijah Wood on the FX dark comedy Wilfred. She originally enrolled at UCLA in a pre-medical track before switching her major to theater after falling in love with acting.
