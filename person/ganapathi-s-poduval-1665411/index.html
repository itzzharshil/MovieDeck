Ganapathi S. Poduval

  • Actor

Ganapathi S. Poduval is an Indian film actor, writer & dubbing artist known for his works in the Malayalam film industry. He is well-known for his roles as child artist in the films "Vinodayathra" (2007) and "Pranchiyettan & The Saint" (2010). Growing up, he went on to star in 2 humongous blockbusters "Jan. E. Man" (2021) & "Manjummel Boys" (2024) both directed by his brother Chidambaram Poduval.

Born in
Payyanur, Kannur, Kerala, India
