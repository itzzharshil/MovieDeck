Hannah Ware (born December 8, 1982) is an English model/actress. She is known for her roles in the films Cop Out and Shame. She also portrayed Emma Kane, the estranged addict daughter of Chicago mayor Tom Kane, in the STARZ series Boss.

She is the daughter of John Ware, a British journalist and presenter on the BBC public affairs documentary program Panorama. Her sister is singer/songwriter Jessie Ware.
