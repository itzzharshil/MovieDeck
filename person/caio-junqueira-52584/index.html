Caio de Lima Torres Junqueira (Rio de Janeiro, November 15, 1976 – Rio de Janeiro, January 23, 2019) was a Brazilian actor. He was the son of actor Fábio Junqueira, who died in 2008, and the brother of actor Jonas Torres.
