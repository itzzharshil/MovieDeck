David Henrie (born July 11, 1989) is an American actor, writer, and director. He is noted for playing Ted Mosby's future son on How I Met Your Mother, Larry on That's So Raven and Justin Russo on Wizards of Waverly Place as well as starring in the films Little Boy, Walt Before Mickey and Reagan.
