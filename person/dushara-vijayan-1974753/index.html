Dushara Vijayan

  • Actress
  • 14 titles
  • Born 17 October 1997

Dushara Vijayan is an Indian Model and Film Actress, who works in Tamil film industry. She got her first major breakthrough after starring in "Sarpatta Parambarai" (2021). She then went on to play Dhanush's sister in "Raayan" & a pivotal role beside Superstar Rajinikanth in "Vettaiyan" both films released in 2024 became one of the highest grossers of that year in Tamil Nadu. Dushara was paired with Chiyaan Vikram in "Veera Dheera Sooran Part-2" (2025).

Chennai, Tamilnadu, India
