Shreya Ghoshal was born on March 12, 1984 in Rawatbhata, Rajasthan, India. She is known for her work on 3 Idiots (2009), Raanjhanaa (2013) and My Name Is Khan (2010). She has been married to Shiladitya Mukhopadhyaya since February 5, 2015.
