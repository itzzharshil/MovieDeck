Bebe Wood

Bebe Wood (born 8 August 2001) is an American actress and singer. She is known for her roles as Shania in the NBC television series The New Normal, as Shannon on the ABC television series The Real O'Neals, Gretchen Wieners in Mean Girls, and as Lake Meriwether on the Hulu Original series Love, Victor.
