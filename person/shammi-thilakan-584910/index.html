Shammi Thilakan

  • Actor
  • 104 titles

Shammi Thilakan is an Indian film actor who is active in Malayalam films.

He is the son of late actor Thilakan. He lent his voice for Napoleon in Devasuram, Prem Nazir in Kadathanadan Ambadi and for Nassar in Ghazal, in which he won state award for best dubbing artist.

Born in
Pathanamthitta, Kerala, India
