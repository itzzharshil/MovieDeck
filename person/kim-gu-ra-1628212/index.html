Kim Gu-ra (김구라), born Kim Hyun-dong (김현동), is a South Korean comedian, broadcaster, and MC in Korea.

After his debut as a comedian for the second SBS recruitment in 1993, he continued to live in obscurity and entered the terrestrial broadcasting in October 2004 through Internet broadcasting.
