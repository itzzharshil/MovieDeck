Jung Jee-hyun

Jung Jee-hyun (정지현) is a South Korean television director known for his stylish cinematography and emotionally compelling storytelling. He gained widespread acclaim for directing the hit tvN coming-of-age drama "Twenty Five Twenty One" (2022) and the mystery-thriller "Lies Hidden in My Garden" (2023). His directorial work also includes "You Are My Spring" (2021), as well as co-directing major series such as "The King: Eternal Monarch" (2020) and "Search: WWW" (2019).
