Lilly Krug is a German film and television actress and social media personality. She followed in the footsteps of her mother, actress Veronica Ferres. She is the daughter of Ferres and Martin Krug. Her stepfather is Carsten Maschmeyer. Born in Germany, she resides in Los Angeles where she is studying Psychology. Wikipedia
