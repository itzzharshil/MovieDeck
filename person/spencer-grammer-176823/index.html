Spencer Grammer

Spencer Karen Grammer (born October 9, 1983) is an American actress and voice-over actress best known for her roles as the voice of Summer Smith in the Adult Swim animated science-fiction series Rick and Morty and as Casey Cartwright in the ABC Family college comedy-drama series Greek.

Her father is actor Kelsey Grammer.
