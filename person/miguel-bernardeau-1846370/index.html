Miguel Bernardeau

Miguel Ángel Bernardeau Duato (born 12 December 1996) is a Spanish actor best known for his breakout role as Guzmán Nunier Osuna in the Netflix teen drama series Élite. He is the son of television producer Miguel Ángel Bernardeau Maestro and the actress Ana Duato. He studied Dramatic Art in the United States at Santa Monica College and acting at the American Academy of Dramatic Arts in Los Angeles, California.
