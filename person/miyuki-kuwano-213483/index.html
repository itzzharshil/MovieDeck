Miyuki Kuwano

Miyuki Kuwano (桑野美雪) was born on July 17, 1942 in Yokohama, Japan. She is an actress, known for Three Outlaw Samurai (1964), Equinox Flower (1958) and Red Beard (1965). She is the daughter of Michiko Kuwano.
