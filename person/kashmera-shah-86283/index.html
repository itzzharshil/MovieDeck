Kashmera Shah

  • Actress

  • Born 2 December 1970

Kashmera Shah (born 2 December 1970) is an Indian actress and model. She is the Granddaughter of famous Hindustani classical singer, Anjanibai Lolekar. She has appeared in a number of Hindi and Marathi films. She is a half Maharashtrian and half Gujarati. She has also won many beauty contests such as Miss University World and Miss India talent winner.
