Surbhi Chandna

  • Actress
  • 8 titles
  • Born 11 September 1989

Surbhi Chandna is an actress, known for Ishqbaaaz (2016), Sanjivani (2019) .Chandna made her television debut in 2009 with SAB TV's Taarak Mehta Ka Ooltah Chashmah where she played Sweety.In 2013, she played Suzanne in Star Plus's Ek Nanad Ki Khushiyon Ki Chaabi… Meri Bhabhi.From 2014 to 2015, Chandna played Haya, a deaf woman, in Zee TV's Qubool Hai. She made her film debut with Bobby Jasoos where she played Aamna Khan.
