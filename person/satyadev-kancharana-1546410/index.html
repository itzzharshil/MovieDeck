Satyadev Kancharana (born 4 July 1988), is an Indian actor who works predominantly in Telugu cinema and has also appeared in Hindi films.

He began his acting career with supporting roles in films such as *Mr. Perfect* (2011), *Seethamma Vakitlo Sirimalle Chettu* (2013), and *Kshanam* (2016). He was later cast in Puri Jagannadh's *Jyothi Lakshmi* (2015). His subsequent films include *Bluff Master* (2018), *Brochevarevarura* (2019), *iSmart Shankar* (2019), and Venkatesh Maha's drama *Uma Maheswara Ugra Roopasya* (2020).

Satyadev later appeared in *Thimmarusu* (2021), *Skylab* (2021), the political action thriller *Godfather* (2022), in which he played the antagonist, the Hindi action-adventure film *Ram Setu* (2022), *Krishnamma* (2024), *Zebra* (2024), and *Rao Bahadur* (2026).
