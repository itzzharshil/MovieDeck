Avneet Kaur

  • Actress
  • 12 titles

Avneet Kaur is an Indian actress. She started her career with Dance India Dance Li'l Masters as a contestant. She then participated in Dance Ke Superstars. Kaur made her acting debut with Meri Maa, playing the character Jhilmil. Later, she was part of Tedhe Hain Par Tere Mere Hain.
