Sshivada Nair is a film actress, who has appeared in Tamil and Malayalam films. Sshivada was born as Sreelekha Nair in Tiruchirappalli, Tamil Nadu. After fifth standard her family moved to Kerala. She is married to malayalam actor Murali Krishnan.
