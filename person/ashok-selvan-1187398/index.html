Ashok Selvan

Ashok Selvan (born 8 November 1989) is a Indian actor who appears in Tamil films. He made his breakthrough playing one of the lead roles in Soodhu Kavvum (2013), before gaining critical acclaim for his performances in C. V. Kumar's productions Pizza 2: The Villa and Thegidi (2014).

Born in
Erode, Tamil Nadu, India
