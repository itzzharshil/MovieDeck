H. Vinoth

  • Director
  • 8 titles
  • Born 5 September 1973

H. Vinoth is an Indian film writer and director who made his directorial debut with Sathuranga Vettai (2014) next venture Theeran Adhigaaram Ondru with actor Karthi was the biggest blockbuster of that year.

Vinoth's debut was produced by actor, director Manobala and was distributed by Thirupathi Brothers Film Media. He also directed three films with Ajith Kumar (Nerkonda Paarvai, Valimai, Thunivu). He is currently working on Jana Nayagan with Vijay.
