Vedang Raina is a Bollywood actor. Besides his charming looks, Vedang is also popular for his singing abilities. Born and raised in Mumbai, he attended Jamnabai Narsee School and later pursued a business degree at the Narsee Monjee Institute of Management Studies (NMIMS) in Mumbai. Despite his business education, he harboured a natural talent and interest in acting. His journey began with participating in plays during his school and college days. Initially known for his musical talents, singing, and playing the guitar at inter-college events, he later ventured into the world of acting.
