Abubakr Ali

Abubakr Ali (born June 20, 1991) is an Egyptian-born American actor. He played Officer Samir Bashir in Law & Order: Organized Crime on television and the lead in the 2022 film Anything's Possible. Ali was born in Egypt to a Muslim family and moved to the United States in 2000 where he was raised in Pasadena, California.
