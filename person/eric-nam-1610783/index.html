Eric Nam is an American singer, songwriter, and television personality based in South Korea. Since his debut in 2013, he was named GQ Korea's Man of the Year in 2016, included on Forbes' 30 Under 30 Asia in 2017, and named as one of YouTube Music's Global Trending Artists on the Rise in 2019.
