Sherri Shepherd

  • Actress
  • 61 titles
  • Born 22 April 1967

Sherri Evonne Shepherd is an American comedienne, actress, and television personality. She is one of five co-hosts on the ABC daytime talkshow, The View, as well as being the current host of the Newlywed Game. As an actress, she had a recurring role as Angie Jordan on the NBC series 30 Rock, starred in the sitcom Less Than Perfect and her own sitcom Sherri on Lifetime.
