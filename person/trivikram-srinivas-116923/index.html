Trivikram Srinivas

  • Director
  • 39 titles
  • Born 7 November 1971

Trivikram Srinivas is an Indian film director, screenwriter and dialogue writer renowned for his work in Telugu cinema. He is celebrated for his witty dialogues, philosophical undercurrents and emotionally resonant family dramas. Starting out as a dialogue writer on hits like Swayamvaram (1999), Nuvve Kavali (2000), Nuvvu Naaku Nachav (2001), Manmadhudu (2002) and Malliswari (2004), he transitioned to directing with Nuvve Nuvve (2002) and went on to make acclaimed and commercially successful films such as Athadu (2005), Jalsa (2008), Julayi (2012), Atharintiki Daaredi (2013), S/O Satyamurthy (2015) , A Aa (2016) and Ala Vaikunthapurramuloo (2020). Trivikram has received multiple Nandi Awards and Filmfare Awards for his writing and direction.
