Nyra Banerjee

  • Actress
  • 25 titles

Nyra Banerjee, previously known mononymously as Madhuurima, is an Indian film actress. Following appearances in various television serials, she made her feature film debut in Priyadarshan's Kammal Dhamaal Malamaal, which was an average success and then went on to act in several successful Telugu films.
