Jiang Peiyao

  • Actress
  • 30 titles
  • Born 10 March 1994

Jiang Pei Yao is a mainland Chinese actress who graduated from the undergraduate program of the Acting Department at Beijing Film Academy and studies in the Class of '16 Master's program at Beijing Film Academy. In 2014, she made her debut as Zhang Jiaqi in the comedy film "Dandy Diary".
