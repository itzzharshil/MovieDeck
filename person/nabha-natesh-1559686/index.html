Nabha Natesh (born December 11, 1995) is an Indian actress and model who works predominantly in Telugu and Kannada cinema. She made her feature film acting debut in the Kannada action film *Vajrakaya* (2015), followed by the action thriller *Lee* (2017).

Natesh made her Telugu cinema debut in the romantic comedy *Nannu Dochukunduvate* (2018), earning a nomination for the SIIMA Award for Best Female Debut – Telugu. She gained wider recognition the following year starring in the science fiction action film *iSmart Shankar* (2019). Her subsequent Telugu releases include *Disco Raja* (2020), the romantic comedy *Solo Brathuke So Better* (2020), *Alludu Adhurs* (2021), and the black comedy crime thriller *Maestro* (2021).

In 2024, she starred in the comedy drama *Darling*.
