Gautami Tadimalla

  • Actress
  • 86 titles
  • Born 2 July 1968

Gautami Tadimalla is an Indian film actress who has worked mainly in Tamil films, although she also appeared in successful films in Telugu, Malayalam, Hindi and Kannada cinema. She is also a television actress, television host and decent costume designer.

Born in
Hyderabad, Telangana, India
