Park In-je

Park In-je (박인제) is a South Korean film and television director and screenwriter born on May 21, 1973. A graduate of the Korea National University of Arts, he began his career directing short films before making his feature film directorial debut with the conspiracy thriller Moby Dick (2011).

Park subsequently wrote and directed the political drama film The Mayor (2017) and co-directed the second season of the hit Netflix original series Kingdom (2020). He reached global prominence as the main director of the Disney+ original series Moving (2023), which earned top accolades including the Grand Prize at the Baeksang Arts Awards and Blue Dragon Series Awards.
