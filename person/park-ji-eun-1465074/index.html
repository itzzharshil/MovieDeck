Park Ji-eun

  • Writer
  • 14 titles
  • Born 1 January 1976

Park Ji-eun (born 1976) is a South Korean television screenwriter. She wrote the hit Korean dramas Queen of Housewives, My Husband Got a Family, My Love from the Star, The Producers, The Legend of the Blue Sea, Crash Landing on You, and Queen of Tears.
