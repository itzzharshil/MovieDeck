Park Jung-woo

  • Actor
  • 7 titles
  • Born 19 January 1996

Park Jung-woo (박정우) is a South Korean actor born on January 19, 1996. He began his acting career in web dramas, gaining initial notice for his main role in the hit series Love Playlist (2017–2019).

Park expanded his screen work into mainstream television and film, notably starring as Baek Hyun-jin in the Netflix feature film 20th Century Girl (2022). His television credits include supporting roles and appearances in series such as Hospital Playlist (2020–2021), D.P. (2021), and Fly High Butterfly (2022).
