Julia Montes

Julia Montes (born Mara Hautea Schnittka on 19 March 1995) is a Filipino actress. She is best known for her roles in the films Way Back Home (2011), A Moment In Time (2013), Halik Sa Hangin (2015), Five Breakups and a Romance (2023), and Topakk (2024).
