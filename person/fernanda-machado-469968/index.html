Fernanda Machado

  • Actress
  • 17 titles
  • Born 10 October 1980

Fernanda Arrias Machado (Maringá, October 10, 1980) is a former Brazilian actress. She is nationally known for playing Maria, a law student and girlfriend of the protagonist André Mathias in the film Elite Squad, the ambitious Dalila in Soul Mate, the sweet Joana in Tropical Paradise, and the con artist Leila in Love of Life.
