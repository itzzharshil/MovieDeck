Adinath Kothare

  • Actor
  • 24 titles
  • Born 13 May 1984

Adinath Kothare is a Marathi movie actor, director, Film and Television Content Producer. Adinath is the son of famous Marathi actor, director, producer Mahesh Kothare. Adinath started his career as a child in the 1994 Marathi film ‘Maza Chakula’. He is well-known for movies such as Zapatlela – 2, Anvatt. He had worked with Pritish Nandy Communications Ltd. as a associate executive producer and with PVR as a Management Trainee- Film Marketing.
