Kenix Kwok Ho-ying

  • Actress
  • 35 titles
  • Born 27 November 1969

Kenix Kwok Ho-ying is a Hong Kong actress with family roots in Zhongshan, Guangdong, China. Along with Maggie Cheung Ho-yee, Ada Choi Siu-fan, Flora Chan Wai-han and Jessica Hsuan Huen, Kenix is known as one of the Top 5 "Fa Dans" of TVB from the mid-1990s to mid-2000s.
