Philip Belmont Cameli (born February 28, 1998) is an American actor. He made his acting debut with a minor guest appearance on Empire (2018).

Cameli started gaining traction starring in projects such as Saved by the Bell (2020-2021), Along for the Ride (2022), and Until Dawn (2025). He is widely recognized as Garrett Graham in the hit Prime Video series Off Campus (2026-present).
