Midhun Manuel Thomas

  • Writer
  • 17 titles
  • Born 18 September 1983

Midhun Manuel Thomas is an Indian film director and screenwriter who works in the Malayalam film industry.

He began his career in the Malayalam film industry in 2014 as the scriptwriter of the film Ohm Shanthi Oshaana. His directorial debut movie was 2015's Aadu. Later he directed Annmariya Kalippilaanu (2016), Alamara (2017), Aadu 2 (2017) and Argentina Fans Kaattoorkadavu (2019), Anjaam Pathiraa (2020).

Born in
Sultan Bathery, Wayanad, Kerala, India
