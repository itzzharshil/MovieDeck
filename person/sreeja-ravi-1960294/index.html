Sreeja Ravi

  • Actress
  • 39 titles

Sreeja Ravi is an Indian voice artist who has lent her dubbing voice to over 1500 films in total and also performed voices for numerous commercial ads. She started her dubbing career in the year 1975 for the movie Utharayanam, directed by G. Aravindan. She can speak English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Malayalam and also Kannada, as she used those six major Indian languages to perform dubbing roles in foreign productions.

Born in
Kannur, Kerala, India
