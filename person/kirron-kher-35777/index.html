Kirron Kher (born June 14, 1955) is an Indian actress, singer, television personality and politician. She was born in Bangalore to a Punjabi Sikh family and grew up in Chandigarh. She primarily acts in Hindi and Punjabi films and series. She is best known for her roles in Bariwali (1999), Om Shanti Om (2007), and Khoobsurat (2014). She is also known for her role as a judge in the popular talent show, India's Got Talent, since 2009.
