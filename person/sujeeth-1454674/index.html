Sujeeth Reddy is a Telugu Indian film director and writer. He made his directorial debut in the Telugu film industry with the romantic comedy thriller film Run Raja Run at the age of 23. He directed about 38 short films before entering Telugu cinema. Prior to films, Sujeeth started to study to be a chartered accountant, but eventually quit to pursue his passion for filmmaking.
