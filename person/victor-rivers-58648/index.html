Victor Rivers (born Victor Rivas in Sancti Spíritus, Cuba) is a Cuban-American actor, known for The Mask of Zorro (1998), Blood In, Blood Out (1993) and Hulk (2003). He has been married to Mim Eichler Rivas since 1988. They have one child.
