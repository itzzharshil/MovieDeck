Roshan Meka

  • Actor
  • 5 titles
  • Born 13 March 1999

Roshan Meka is an Indian actor who works in Telugu films. He is the son of actors Srikanth and Sivaranjini. He made his debut in a lead role with Nirmala Convent and went on to win SIIMA Award for Best Male Debut – Telugu. He later played the lead in Pelli SandaD alongside Sreeleela.
