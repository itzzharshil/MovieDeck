DEMIAN, born Sohn Jeong Hyuck, is a South Korean singer-songwriter. He made his debut on March, 11th, 2020 with the single "Cassette".

In 2021, he participated in the second season of JTBC's music program "Super Band".

In 2023, he signed an exclusive contract with MS Team Entertainment.
