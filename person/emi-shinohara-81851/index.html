Emi Shinohara

  • Actress
  • 117 titles
  • Born 8 August 1963

Emi Shinohara (born Emiko Shinohara on August 8, 1963) was a Japanese voice actress employed by 81 Produce. She was born in Fukushima Prefecture and raised in Nagano. She is married to tokusatsu actor Hiroshi Watari. She's known for playing Sailor Jupiter in the original Sailor Moon anime.
