Sergio Bini Bustric

Sergio Bini, Bustric in art, graduated from the Faculty of Letters and Philosophy University of Bologna. He attended the National Circus School in Paris Annie Fratellini and Pierre Etaix and the abstract of pantomime with Etienne Decroux in Rome and the school of Roy Bosier. Then a period of study with Jon Strasberg "Actors Studio". After working in experimental theater he founded, along with three other youths, the Company Melquiades
