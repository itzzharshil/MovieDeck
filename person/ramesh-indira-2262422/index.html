**Ramesh Indira** is an accomplished Indian film director, screenwriter, and actor who works predominantly in Kannada cinema. Known for his multifaceted contributions to the industry, transitioning successfully from television direction and creative roles to feature filmmaking and character acting, he has established a strong and respected presence in Sandalwood.

Born and raised in Bengaluru, Karnataka, Ramesh built a solid foundation in the television and media landscape. He worked extensively in Kannada television as a prominent director and creative head associated with Shruti Naidu Film Production, helming popular daily serials such as Mahadevi. His deep understanding of narrative pacing and character driven storytelling on television eventually paved the way for his transition to the silver screen.

He made his feature film directorial debut with the critically acclaimed 2019 emotional comedy drama Premier Padmini, starring Jaggesh, Sudha Rani, and Pramod Panju. The film won widespread praise for its sensitive handling of modern urban relationships, witty dialogue, and strong performances, instantly marking him as a promising filmmaker in Sandalwood.

Following his directorial success, Ramesh expanded his creative portfolio into acting, where he quickly gained massive recognition for playing intense, menacing, and memorable character roles. He made a strong impact as Suryanarayana in Prashanth Neel's blockbuster action epic K.G.F: Chapter 2 (2022). He further solidified his reputation as a formidable character actor with his chilling portrayal of the primary antagonist in Hemanth M. Rao’s acclaimed romantic drama Sapta Sagaradaache Ello Side B (2023).
