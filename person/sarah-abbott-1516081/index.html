Sarah Abbott

  • Actress
  • 14 titles
  • Born 22 January 2006

Sarah Abbott who has gained fame for her career in film and TV. She was cast to appear in the 2019 horror film The Silence, as well as on the TV series Black Mirror in 2017. Abbott made her first on-screen appearance in 2014 on the TV series Working the Engels. In 2023, she played Sadie in an episode of the TV series Transplant. She is also known for Ginny & Georgia (2021), Feel the Beat (2020) and Black Mirror (2011).
