Kareena Kapoor Khan (born September 21, 1980) is an Indian actress and producer who works predominantly in Hindi cinema. She made her acting debut in *Refugee* (2000), winning the Filmfare Award for Best Female Debut. Her early career included roles in *Kabhi Khushi Kabhie Gham...* (2001), *Chameli* (2003), *Dev* (2004), and *Omkara* (2006).

She won the Filmfare Award for Best Actress for the romantic comedy *Jab We Met* (2007). Over the following years, her releases included *3 Idiots* (2009), *Golmaal 3* (2010), *Bodyguard* (2011), *Ra.One* (2011), and *Talaash* (2012). Her subsequent credits include *Bajrangi Bhaijaan* (2015), *Udta Punjab* (2016), *Veere Di Wedding* (2018), *Good Newwz* (2019), and *Laal Singh Chaddha* (2022).

In streaming, Kapoor Khan debuted in the thriller *Jaane Jaan* (2023), followed by *Crew* (2024) and *Singham Again* (2024). She expanded into production as co-producer and lead of *The Buckingham Murders* (2024).
