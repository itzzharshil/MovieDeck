Choi Min-yeong

Choi Min-yeong (최민영) is a South Korean actor born on March 31, 2002, in Seoul, South Korea. He began his acting career in 2014 as a child performer in stage musicals and television series.

Choi established himself as a talented young actor by portraying the younger counterparts of male leads in hit Korean dramas such as Strong Girl Bong-soon (2017), Mr. Sunshine (2018), Itaewon Class (2020), and Twenty-Five Twenty-One (2022). He gained international acclaim starring as Dae in the Netflix teen romance series XO, Kitty (2023). On the big screen, his performance in the film Dream Palace (2023) earned him the Best New Actor award at the Buil Film Awards.
