Yuki Furukawa is a Japanese actor who gained popularity as Naoki Irie in the 2013 J-Drama Mischievous Kiss: Love in Tokyo. He also portrayed Kensei Odaka/Kamen Rider Nox from Kamen Rider Zeztz.
