Shaheer Sheikh (born 26 March 1984) is an Indian actor and model. He is best known for playing Dev Dixit in Kuch Rang Pyar Ke Aise Bhi, Anant Bajpai in Navya..Naye Dhadkan Naye Sawaal, Abir Rajvansh in Yeh Rishtey Hain Pyaar Ke and warrior Arjun in Mahabharat.
