Santhosh Prathap (born 2 June 1987) is an Indian actor working in the Tamil film industry. His feature film debut was in the lead role Kathai Thiraikathai Vasanam Iyakkam (2014). In the Tamil thriller series The Game: You Never Play Alone (2025), Santhosh Prathap plays a video game developer.
