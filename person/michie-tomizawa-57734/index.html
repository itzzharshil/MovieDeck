Michie Tomizawa (富沢 美智恵, former stage name: 富沢 美智江, Tomizawa Michie, October 20, 1961) is a Japanese voice actress, actress, singer, and narrator. She was born in Ueda City, Nagano Prefecture and raised in Takasaki City, Gunma Prefecture. She is affiliated with Aoni Production.
