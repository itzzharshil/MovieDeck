Tian Xiwei

  • Actress
  • 23 titles
  • Born 14 October 1997

Tian Xiwei is a Chinese actress born in Chongqing, China. She graduated from Shanghai Theatre Academy and officially entered showbiz in 2017 with her role in the 2018 series "Waiting for You in a Long Time". Tian gained widespread recognition for her role as Li Wei in the popular drama New Life Begins (2022). Her other notable works include Romance on the Farm (2023), Wrong Carriage, Right Groom (2023), and Guardians of the Dafeng (2024), where she portrayed Princess Lin'an.
