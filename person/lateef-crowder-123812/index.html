Lateef Crowder Dos Santos (born November 23, 1977) is a Brazilian-born American actor, stunt performer and Capoeira practitioner. As a member of the ZeroGravity stunt team since 2000, he has been featured in multiple internet short videos and demo reels, such as Inmate 451.
