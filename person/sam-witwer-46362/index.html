Sam Witwer

  • Actor
  • 40 titles
  • Born 20 October 1977

Samuel Stewart Witwer is an American actor, voice actor, and musician. He is known for playing Crashdown in Battlestar Galactica, Davis Bloome in Smallville, Aidan Waite in Being Human, Mr. Hyde in Once Upon a Time, and Starkiller in the Star Wars: The Force Unleashed video game series.
