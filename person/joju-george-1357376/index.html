Joju George

  • Actor
  • 129 titles
  • Born 22 October 1977

Jojo George is a Malayalam film actor known for his performance in supporting and comedy roles. He made his debut through Pattallam(2003) directed by Lal Jose. After this, he got a handful of roles in several other movies such as Pattalam, Vaasthavam, Best Actor, Kili Poyi, Neram, Beautiful, Trivandrum Lodge, 1983, Hotel California, Pullipulikalum Aattinkuttiyum, RajadhiRaja, Cousins,[2] Onnam Loka Mahayudham, Oru Second Class Yathra and Lukka Chuppi

Thrissur, Kerala, India
