Zain Al Rafeea

  • Actor
  • 3 titles
  • Born 10 October 2004

Zain Al Rafeea is a Syrian-born actor known for his role in the 2018 film Capernaum. He was born on October 10, 2004, in Daraa, Syria, and later moved to Lebanon due to the Syrian conflict. Discovered by director Nadine Labaki, he played the lead role in Capernaum, earning international acclaim. In 2018, he relocated to Norway with his family. In 2021, he appeared in the Marvel film Eternals.
