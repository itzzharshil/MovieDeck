Tavia Yeung

  • Actress
  • 58 titles
  • Born 30 August 1979

Tavia Yeung is a Hong Kong actress born 30 August 1979. She has starred in numerous popular Hong Kong television series such as Vigilante Force, Twin of Brothers, Dicey Business, Heart of Greed, Moonlight Resonance, Beyond the Realm of Conscience, The Mysteries of Love, The Other Truth, The Hippocratic Crush, and Silver Spoon, Sterling Shackles. On October 26, 2020, she announced on Instagram that she was changing her name from Yeung Yi (楊怡) to Yeung Sin-yiu (楊茜堯).

Born in
Zhanjiang, Guangdong, China
