Park Sung-woong

Park Sung-woong (박성웅) is a South Korean actor. He made his acting debut in 1997 gangster comedy film "No. 3". He's married to Shin Eun-jung, whom he met while filming "The Legend", in which the characters they portray were lovers.

Born in
Chungju, North Chungcheong Province, South Korea
