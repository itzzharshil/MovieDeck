Madeleine Martin

Madeleine Martin (born April 15, 1993) is an American actress. She is known for voicing JoJo Tickle, the title character in the animated TV series JoJo's Circus. She also portrayed the character Rebecca "Becca" Moody on Showtime comedy-drama Californication.
