Sarinrat Thomas

Sarinrat Thomas, nicknamed Jear, is a drama therapist, actress, acting coach, model, MC and self-development workshop facilitator. She graduated and qualified as a drama therapist in the UK in 2000 and has a Master degree in Psychotherapy and Counselling. Sarinrar moved to York, UK in 2017. She acted as an operating nurse in the film The Impossible (2012).
