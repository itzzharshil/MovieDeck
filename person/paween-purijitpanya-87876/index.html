Paween Purijitpanya (1978, Thailand) has been fascinated by film and visual effects from a young age. He studied film and photography at the Chulalongkorn University in Thailand. He has made numerous music videos for well-known Thai bands; some received awards. Purijitpanya is present at IFFR 2009 with Body and an episode in 4BIA.
