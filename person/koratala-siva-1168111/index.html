Koratala Siva

Koratala Siva(born 15th June 1975) is an Indian film director and screenwriter who works primarily in Telugu cinema. He worked as dialogue and script writer for the films Bhadra and dialogue writer for Munna, Okkadunnadu, Brindavanam and Ossaravelli. Best known for directing Mirchi. Currently Siva is all set to direct South Superstar Mahesh Babu in his upcoming film which will start shooting in 2014 and produced by UTV Motion Pictures.
