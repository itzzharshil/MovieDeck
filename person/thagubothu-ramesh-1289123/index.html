Ramesh Ramilla, better known as Thagubothu Ramesh, is an Indian film actor and comedian who primarily works with Telugu cinema. He got the moniker "Tagubothu" as he portrayed several roles as a drunkard. He rose to prominence with films such as Mahatma, Ala Modalaindi, Pilla Zamindar and Ishq.
