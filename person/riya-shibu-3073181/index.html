Riya Shibu

Riya Shibu (born September 25, 2004) is an Indian actress and producer who predominantly works in the Malayalam and Tamil film industries. She entered the film industry at a young age and has been active both on screen and behind the scenes

She made her acting debut in the Malayalam film Cup, appearing in a lead role. Her breakthrough came with Sarvam Maya, which brought her wider recognition and established her as a notable young presence in the industry

Alongside acting, Riya is also a producer under the banner Thameens Films and its distribution arm, HR Films. As a producer, her credits include Thugs (2023), Mumbaikar (2023), Mura (2024), and Veera Dheera Sooran: Part 2 (2025), reflecting her growing involvement in South Indian cinema
