Shane Nigam

  • Actor
  • 29 titles
  • Born 21 December 1995

Shane Nigam is an Indian actor known for his work in Malayalam cinema. He began his career as a child artist before earning acclaim for his breakout role in Kismath (2016). Shane is recognized for his naturalistic performances in films such as Parava (2017), Eeda (2018), Kumbalangi Nights (2019), Ishq (2019) and RDX: Robert Dony Xavier (2023). Balancing intense and understated roles, he has emerged as one of the most distinctive voices among a new generation of Malayalam actors.
