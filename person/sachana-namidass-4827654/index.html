Sachana Namidass

  • Actress
  • 6 titles
  • Born 1 May 2003

Sachana Namidass is an emerging Indian actress primarily known for her work in the Tamil film industry and her participation in reality television. She gained significant recognition for her role as Jothi (Ammu), the daughter of Vijay Sethupathi's character, in the critically acclaimed 2024 film Maharaja. Beyond films, she rose to wider fame as a contestant on Bigg Boss Tamil Season 8 in late 2024.
