Iman Benson (born June 25, 2000) is an American actress from Atlanta, Georgia. She is best known for her roles as Tia Russell in the sitcom Uncle Buck (2016), Reagan in the sitcom Alexa & Katie (2018-2020) and Ilonka in the television series The Midnight Club (2022).
