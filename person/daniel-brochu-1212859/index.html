Daniel Brochu was born on 28 February 1970 in Montreal, Quebec, Canada. He is an actor, known for Arthur (1996), To Catch a Killer (2023) and Mr. Nobody (2009). He has been married to Sara Bradeen since 2 July 2005. They have two children.
