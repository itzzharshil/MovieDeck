Kim Young-dae

  • Actor
  • 14 titles
  • Born 2 March 1996

Kim Young-dae is a South Korean actor and model under the Agency Outer Korea. His acting debut was in 2018 in the web drama "Office Watch 2". Since then, he has appeared in a variety of television dramas and was nominated for Best New Actor in the 2019 MBC Drama Awards for the drama "Extraordinary You".
