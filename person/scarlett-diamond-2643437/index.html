Scarlett Diamond

  • Actress
  • 8 titles

Scarlett Diamond appeared in her first movie "Sacred Vow" at the tender age of one. She expressed an interest in acting at an early age which is not surprising as her grandpa, Rob Diamond, is an award winning filmmaker. She went on to land a small speaking part in the family drama "Our Father's Keeper" and was absolutely adorable on screen. More recently, she booked her first starring role opposite Vincent Vargas from "The Mayans M.C" in the feature film "Lucy Shimmers and The Prince of Peace." With her larger than life personality and infectious smile, Scarlett is a true natural talent. Look for this rising star to grace us on the big screen for many years to come.
