José Tenoch Huerta Mejía (Spanish: [teˈnotʃˈweɾta]; born January 29, 1981) is a Mexican actor and activist. He has appeared in several movies in Latin America and Spain, starring in feature films, short films, and Narcos: Mexico, credited as Tenoch Huerta. He is featured in Mónica Maristain's 30 Actors Made in Mexico book. He plays Namor in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), starting with the film Black Panther: Wakanda Forever (2022), in which he is credited as Tenoch Huerta Mejía.
