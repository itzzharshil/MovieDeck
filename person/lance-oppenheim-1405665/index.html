Lance Oppenheim (born January 26, 1996; Fort Lauderdale) is an American filmmaker, documentarian, producer, and multi-instrumentalist from Fort Lauderdale, Florida. His work blends cinematic genres to explore the lives of people who create homes in unconventional spaces and places. His debut feature Some Kind of Heaven (2020) was an Official Selection at the 2020 Sundance Film Festival. Lance has gone on to make his first feature length film, Primetime (2026) which was a box office success by the first weekend.
