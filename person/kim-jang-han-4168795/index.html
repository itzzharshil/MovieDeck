Kim Jang-han (김장한) is a South Korean television director. He gained initial recognition with the Wavve web series You Raise Me Up (2021).

He later co-directed the hit SBS fantasy-romance drama My Demon (2023–2024) alongside Kwon Da-som. His subsequent work includes directing the Netflix romantic-comedy series Our Sticky Love (2026).
