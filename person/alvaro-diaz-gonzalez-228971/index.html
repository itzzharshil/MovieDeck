Álvaro Díaz González

  • Writer

  • Born 2 January 1972

Álvaro Díaz González is a Chilean journalist, producer, film and television director. He is a member and founder of the independent production company Aplaplac. Together with Pedro Peirano, he is the creator, director, producer, screenwriter, voice actor and puppeteer of the children's series "31 minutos".
