Soori

  • Actor
  • 100 titles
  • Born 27 August 1977

Soori is a Indian film actor who appears in Tamil films. He rose to fame after his role in the film Vennila Kabadi Kuzhu (2009), in which the scene involving a parotta eating challenge earned him the nickname Parotta Soori. He performed mainly comical sidekick roles until later transitioning into a dramatic leading man with films such as Viduthalai (2023), Kottukkaali (2024) and Garudan (2024), earning widespread recognition for his performances in the latter films.
