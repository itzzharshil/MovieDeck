Kang Hye-jung

  • Actress
  • 28 titles
  • Born 4 January 1982

Kang Hye-jung (강혜정) is a South Korean actress, best known for her work in Oldboy (2003) and Welcome to Dongmakgol (2005). Both of these were critically and commercially successful films which earned her prestigious acting awards, including the Grand Bell and Blue Dragon awards. She is married to Daniel Armand Lee (a.k.a. Tablo) of the hip hop group Epik High. The couple has one daughter.
