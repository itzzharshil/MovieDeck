Brandon Paul Flynn (born October 11, 1993) is an American actor known for his role as Justin Foley in the Netflix series 13 Reasons Why (2017–2020). His work in film and television includes the series Manhunt (2024), Ratched (2020), and True Detective (2019), as well as the films The Parenting (2025) and Hellraiser (2022).
