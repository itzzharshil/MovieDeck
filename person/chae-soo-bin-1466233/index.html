Chae Soo-bin

  • Actress
  • 25 titles
  • Born 10 July 1994

Bae Soo-bin, known professionally as Chae Soo-bin, is a South Korean actress. She made her acting debut in the film My Dictator (2014) and later gained attention for her role in the television series Love in the Moonlight (2016). She then transitioned to leading roles with dramas The Rebel (2017) and I'm Not a Robot (2017–2018), before gaining further recognition for her roles in When the Phone Rings (2024) and Take Charge of My Heart (2026).
