Denise Fraga

Denise Rodrigues Fraga (Rio de Janeiro, October 15, 1964) is a Brazilian actress, producer and columnist. She received several awards during her career, including a Grande Otelo, two APCA Awards, a Guarani Award, and two Quality Brazil Awards.
