Asha Negi

  • Actress
  • 11 titles
  • Born 23 August 1989

Asha Negi is an Indian television actress. She is recognised for her roles in the shows Pavitra Rishta as Poorvi, Ek Mutthi Aasmaan as Kalpana, Kuch Toh Hai Tere Mere Darmiyaan as Koyal. She won the Indian dance reality show Nach Baliye 6 along with her partner Rithvik Dhanjani in 2014.

Dehradun, Uttarakhand, India
