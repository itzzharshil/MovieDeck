Michelle Jenner

  • Actress
  • 47 titles
  • Born 14 September 1986

Michelle Jenner Husson (Barcelona, September 14, 1986) is a Spanish film, television, and voice actress. As an actress, she is primarily known for her role as Sara Miranda in the television series Los hombres de Paco, as Keila in Berlin and Berlin and the Lady with an Ermine, and as Isabella I of Castile in the series Isabel. She is also known as a voice actress, providing the Spanish dubbing for British actress Emma Watson, and the voice of Giosuè, the little boy in the film Life Is Beautiful, in addition to dubbing several Pixar and Disney animated films such as Toy Story and The Lion King II: Simba's Pride.
