Nivetha Pethuraj (born 30 November, 1991) is an Indian actress and model who works predominantly in Telugu and Tamil cinema. In 2015, she won the Miss India UAE pageant.

She made her acting debut in the Tamil romantic comedy-drama *Oru Naal Koothu* (2016). She entered Telugu cinema with Vivek Athreya's romantic drama *Mental Madhilo* (2017). Her other film roles include *Tik Tik Tik* (2018), *Thimiru Pudichavan* (2018), *Chitralahari* (2019), *Brochevarevarura* (2019), and *Sangathamizhan* (2019).

Her later film roles include *Ala Vaikunthapurramuloo* (2020), the action thriller *Red* (2021), *Paagal* (2021), *Bloody Mary* (2022), *Virata Parvam* (2022), and *Das Ka Dhamki* (2023). She has also appeared in web series including *Kaala* (2023) and *Paruvu* (2024).
