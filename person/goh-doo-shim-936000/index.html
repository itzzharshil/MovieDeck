Goh Doo-shim

Go Doo-shim (고두심) is an acclaimed South Korean actress born on May 22, 1951, in Jeju City, South Korea. She made her professional acting debut in 1972 and went on to become one of the most celebrated performers in Korean television and film history. Go holds a record seven Grand Prizes (Daesang) across South Korea's major television networks for her powerful dramatic performances. Throughout her multi-decade career, she has starred in iconic television series such as More Beautiful Than a Flower (2004), All About My Mom (2015), My Mister (2018), When the Camellia Blooms (2019), Our Blues (2022), and Twinkling Watermelon (2023). Her notable film credits include My Mother, the Mermaid (2004), Family Ties (2006), Good Morning President (2009), The Preparation (2017), and Exit (2019).
