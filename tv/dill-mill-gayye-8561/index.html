About Dill Mill Gayye

Dill Mill Gayye is an Indian soap medical drama TV series that aired on STAR One. It was the sequel of the television series Sanjivani, which aired on STAR Plus channel. The show ended in October 2010 after running for 3 years. The show was successful amongst the youth of the country that even after so years following its conclusion, the actors of the show like Karan Singh Grover, Shilpa Anand, Jennifer Winget, Sukirti Kandpal, Drashti Dhami, Shweta Gulati and Karan Wahi are still fondly remembered by the people.
