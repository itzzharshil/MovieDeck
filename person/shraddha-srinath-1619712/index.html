Shraddha Srinath (born 29 September 1990) is an Indian actress who works in Tamil, Kannada, Telugu, Hindi, and Malayalam cinema. She previously worked as a corporate real estate lawyer and performed in English theatre before pursuing acting full-time.

After making her screen debut in the Malayalam film *Kohinoor* (2015), Srinath appeared in Pawan Kumar's Kannada psychological thriller *U Turn* (2016), playing an investigative journalist. She later appeared in *Urvi* (2017), *Vikram Vedha* (2017), and *Operation Alamelamma* (2017), for which she received the Filmfare Critics Award for Best Actress – South.

Srinath appeared in Telugu cinema with *Jersey* (2019), in which she played Sarah opposite Nani. Her subsequent films include the legal drama *Nerkonda Paarvai* (2019), *Krishna and His Leela* (2020), *Maara* (2021), *Chakra* (2021), *Irugapatru* (2023), *Saindhav* (2024), *Mechanic Rocky* (2024), and *Daaku Maharaaj* (2025). She has received two Filmfare Awards South and a SIIMA Award.
