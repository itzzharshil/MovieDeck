Penmatsa Subbaraju is an Indian actor who has acted in many Tollywood films in a variety of roles, from negative to supporting characters. His most notable works include Amma Nanna O' Tamil Ammayi, Arya, Pokiri, Leader, Mirchi, and Baahubali 2: The Conclusion.
