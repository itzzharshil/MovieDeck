Guru Somasundaram

  • Actor
  • 55 titles
  • Born 3 September 1975

Guru Somasundaram is an Indian actor who has appeared in Tamil language films. He made his debut in Thiagarajan Kumararaja's Aaranya Kaandam (2011) winning critical acclaim for his role, before also appearing in Suseenthiran's action film Pandiya Naadu (2013), Karthik Subbaraj's gangster film Jigarthanda (2014) and Raju Murugan's political satire film Joker (2016). He is called Kollywood Christian Bale. Now He is Living in Tiruvannamalai.
