Hudson Williams (born February 13, 2001) is a Canadian actor and filmmaker. He has appeared in projects such as Allegiance (2024), All I Need for Christmas (2024), and Tracker (2025). He is best known for his breakout role as Shane Hollander in the Crave original television series Heated Rivalry (2025).
