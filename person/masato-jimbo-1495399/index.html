Masato Jimbo (神保 昌登, Jimbo Masato) is an anime director, animation director, animator, and screenwriter. He is the representative director of PartsCraft.

He became a director after working on studio CAB and Zion (シオン). He often participates in Studio Dean, J.C.STAFF, Doga Kobo and C-Station productions, and recently he often participates in SILVER LINK. Productions.
