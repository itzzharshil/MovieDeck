Oona Chaplin (born Oona Castilla Chaplin, 4 June 1986, Madrid, Spain) is a Spanish-British actress (1.70 m). The daughter of actress Geraldine Chaplin and Chilean cinematographer Patricio Castilla, she is the granddaughter of Charlie Chaplin and great-granddaughter of playwright Eugene O'Neill. Named after her maternal grandmother Oona O'Neill, she attended Gordonstoun School in Scotland before graduating from the Royal Academy of Dramatic Art (RADA) in 2007. She is best known for playing Talisa Maegyr in Game of Thrones (2012–2013, 11 episodes), Zilpha Geary in Taboo (2017, 8 episodes), and Kitty Trevelyan in The Crimson Field (2014). In 2025 she portrayed Varang, the villainous leader of the Ash People, in Avatar: Fire and Ash (2025, dir. James Cameron). ⁠

Her filmography includes: Quantum of Solace (2008) (Daniel Craig, Eva Green); The Devil's Double (2011) (Eddie Marsan, Dougray Scott); What If (2013) (Aubrey Plaza, Miles Teller); Aloft (2014) (Emily Blunt, Gael García Bernal); The Longest Ride (2015) (Scott Eastwood, Britt Robertson); Anchor and Hope (2017) (Jude Law, Keira Knightley); Lullaby (2022) (Josh Hartnett, Jenna Ortega); Avatar: Fire and Ash (2025) (Zoe Saldaña, Sigourney Weaver). TV: The Hour (2011–2012, 12 episodes) (Ben Whishaw, Romola Garai); Sherlock (2012) (Benedict Cumberbatch, Martin Freeman); Game of Thrones (2012–2013) (Emilia Clarke, Kit Harington); Black Mirror (2014) (Jon Hamm, Bryce Dallas Howard); Taboo (2017) (Tom Hiddleston, Jessica Chastain); The Comey Rule (2020) (John Lithgow, Diane Lane); Treason (2022) (Kiefer Sutherland, Olivia Munn). Upcoming: Avatar 4 (2029) and Avatar 5 (2031).
