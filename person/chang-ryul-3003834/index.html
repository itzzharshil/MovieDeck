Chang Ryul

Chang Ryul (born February 14, 1989) is a South Korean actor and model. He is known for his roles in dramas such as Daily Dose of Sunshine, My Name, Stranger Season 2, The Golden Spoon and The Scandal of Chunhwa. He also appeared in films including Master, Detective K: Secret of the Living Dead, and Jo Pil-ho: The Dawning Rage.
