Román Zaragoza

  • Actor
  • 6 titles

Román Zaragoza is a mixed-race actor of Akimel O'otham and Mexican descent on his father's side, and Japanese and Taiwanese descent on his mother's. He has worked with Native Voices at the Autry, the only Native American Equity theatre company in the U.S.
