Aidan Leary

  • Director
  • 9 titles

Aidan Leary is an independent filmmaker with a passion for genre storytelling. He is best known for his debut feature film as a writer/director, Monkey’s Magic Merry Go Round. The film was produced by Joe Swanberg and is being released by Cineverse. As an assistant director, he works primarily in independent horror features. His credits as a 1st AD include Nicolas Curcio's "Play House", Jesse Korman's "Yellow Eyes", Jae Matthews' "Thingy", and Jay Burelson's "Kenneled". He is represented by Thruline Entertainment.
