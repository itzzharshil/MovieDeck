Royce J. Johnson is an American actor, screenwriter, and filmmaker best known for his recurring role as Detective Sergeant Brett Mahoney in the Marvel Cinematic Universe television series Daredevil (2015–2018), Jessica Jones (2015), The Punisher (2017–2019), and Daredevil: Born Again (2026).
