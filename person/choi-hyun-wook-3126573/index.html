Choi Hyun-wook

  • Actor
  • 13 titles
  • Born 30 January 2002

Choi Hyun-wook is a South Korean actor. He began his acting career with a leading role in the web drama Real:Time:Love in 2019 and gained more recognition with roles in 2021 SBS television series Taxi Driver and Racket Boys. He has since become known for starring in Twenty-Five Twenty-One (2022), Weak Hero Class 1 (2022), D.P. 2 (2023), Twinkling Watermelon (2023), My Dearest Nemesis (2025), Notes from the Last Row (2026), and Dead-End Job (2026).
