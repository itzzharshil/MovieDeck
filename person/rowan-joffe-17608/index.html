Born on October 14, 1973, in London, England, Rowan Joffé is a celebrated English screenwriter and director known for his distinctive blend of psychological depth and suspense. The son of famed director Roland Joffé and producer Jane Lapotaire, Rowan started his career honing his craft in television, later making a powerful mark in film and TV dramas.
