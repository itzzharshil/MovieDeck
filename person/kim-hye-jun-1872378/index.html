Kim Hye-jun

  • Actress
  • 20 titles
  • Born 8 May 1995

Kim Hye-jun is a South Korean actress born on May 8, 1995. She debuted in 2015 through the web series "Lily Fever." She gained international recognition for her role as the Queen in the Netflix series "Kingdom." In 2019, she won the Best New Actress award at the Blue Dragon Film Awards for her performance in "Another Child." She also received the Best New Actress (TV) award at the Baeksang Arts Awards in 2022 for her role in "Inspector Koo." Recently, she starred in the 2024 series "A Shop for Killers."
