From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia

Bhuvan Bam (born 22 January 1994) is an Indian comedian, singer, songwriter and YouTube personality from Delhi, India. He is known for his YouTube comedy channel BB Ki Vines. Bhuvan became the first Indian individual YouTube content creator to cross 10 million subscribers in 2018.
