Zhang Zifeng

  • Actress
  • 49 titles
  • Born 27 August 2001

Zhang Zifeng started acting at the age of 5, for commercials at first. In 2009, she was well known by the public for playing the role of "Fang Deng" in his debut film "Aftershock", and won the Best Newcomer Award at the 31st Popular Film Hundred Flowers Award for this film.
