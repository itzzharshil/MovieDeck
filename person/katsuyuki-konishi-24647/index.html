Katsuyuki Konishi

Katsuyuki Konishi (小西 克幸, Konishi Katsuyuki) is a Japanese voice actor and stage actor currently affiliated with Ken Production. He was the vice-chairman of the theater company Hero Q.

Shin Azuma and Hatsu Fujibaku are aliases of Katsuyuki Konishi for adult games and anime.
