Reena

  • Actress
  • 35 titles
  • Born 14 March 1958

Reena is an Indian film actress best known for her work in Malayalam cinema. She was one of the lead actress during the late 1970s. She has acted in more than 100 films. She has acted in few Tamil, Kannada, Telugu movies as well.Currently she is focussing more on Television series.

Reena was born to Peter Reskyuna and Jessy in 1958 at Mangalore. She has a brother, Ivan. She pursued bachelor's degree from Madras Presentation College, Perumbavoor. She is married to Issac. They have two sons, Alan and Abin.

She started her career at 14, in Chukku movie as Sheela's daughter. She started production company, V.I.C and produced movies Druvasankamam and Ente Kadha. Now she is acting in Tele films and soap operas. Currently she is acting in Aniyathi serial in Mazhavil Manorama.
