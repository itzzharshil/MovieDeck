Gopika

  • Actress
  • 27 titles
  • Born 26 August 1984

Gopika is an Indian film actress, who has predominantly starred in Malayalam films.

Starting her career as a model, she ventured into acting with the film Pranayamanithooval (2003). She went on to appear in over 35 films, working in the Tamil, Telugu and Kannada film industries as well. She is well known for her role in Autograph, directed by Cheran.
