Han Jun-hee

Han Jun-hee (한준희) is a South Korean film and television director, writer, and producer. He gained widespread acclaim for directing the Baeksang Award-winning Netflix original series "D.P." (2021–2023) and feature films such as "Coin Locker Girl" (2015) and "Hit-and-Run Squad" (2019). He also served as the Creative Director and Executive Producer for the critically acclaimed youth action series "Weak Hero Class 1" (2022).
