Saurav Chakraborty

  • Actor
  • 11 titles

Sourav Chakraborty is a renowned Indian voice artist best known as the primary voice behind almost all main characters in the popular animated series Motu Patlu, including Motu, Patlu, Dr. Jhatka, Ghasitaram, John the Don, and Inspector Chingum. He has voiced these characters for both Hindi and Bengali dubs since the show's inception. He is an Actor, Stand-Up Comedian, Writer, Voice Over Artist who is fluent in Hindi. As an actor, he has acted in many movies alongside Sonu Sood. He was also a participant in the INDIAN Laughter Challenge and Comedy Circus. He has also played a few different characters in the F.I.R. Serial on Sony SAB Channel. He is a brilliant Mimicry artist and is well known for his Comical Hindi voice behind Motu Patlu, Shaun the Sheep, Oggy and the Cockroaches, and Zig & Sharko. His name is also mentioned as Sourav Chakraborty.

He should not be confused with Sourav Chakraborty, who is a (Bengali Actor/Director/Writer).
