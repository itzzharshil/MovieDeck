Kim Keum-soon

  • Actress
  • 66 titles
  • Born 21 August 1973

Kim Keum-soon is a South Korean actress. She made her debut in 2013. She is represented by Saram Entertainment. She received critical acclaim as the lead in "Jeong Sun" (2022), winning Best Actress at the Rome International Film Festival. In the same year, she was awarded Actress of the Year at the Busan International Film Festival for "Star of Ulsan".

Sokcho, Gangwon, South Korea
