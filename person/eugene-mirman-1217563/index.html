Eugene Mirman (born Evgeniy Borisovich Mirman; July 24, 1974) is a Russian-born American comedian, writer and filmmaker who is best known for his work on the critically acclaimed cable series Flight of the Conchords, the animated show Home Movies, a show produced by Loren Bouchard, and currently voices Gene Belcher, on Bob's Burgers.
