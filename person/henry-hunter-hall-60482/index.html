Henry Hunter Hall (born April 5, 1996) is an American actor. He is is the son of filmmaker and actress Kasi Lemmons and actor Vondie Curtis-Hall. He had a cameo role as a child in his father's 1997 film Gridlock'd, starring Tim Roth and Tupac. In 2019, Hall appeared in the miniseries When They See Us.
