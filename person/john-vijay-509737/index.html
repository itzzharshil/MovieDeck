John Vijay

John Vijay is an Indian Tamil film actor, who played supporting roles. He has mostly played the role a "comedy/comic villain" in Tamil films.

Vijay also works as the head programme director of Radio One FM. John is the chief of 'H2O', an advertising agency with its office at Nungambakkam.
