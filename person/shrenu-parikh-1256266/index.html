Shrenu Parikh

  • Actress
  • 9 titles
  • Born 11 November 1989

Shrenu Parikh is an Indian television actress known for portraying Gauri Kumari Sharma in Ishqbaaaz and Janhvi Mittal in Ek Bhram Sarvagun Sampanna.Parikh began her acting career as Roopa in Star Plus's Gulaal. She then appeared alongside Narendra Jha and Kunal Verma in Colors TV's Havan, portraying Aastha.

In 2012, she played Rajni Vaishnav in Sony TV's romantic comedy Byaah Hamari Bahoo Ka, co-starring Gaurav Khanna. In 2013, she appeared in Star Plus's romantic drama Iss Pyaar Ko Kya Naam Doon?...Ek Baar Phir as Aastha Kirloskar opposite Avinash Sachdev.In 2017, she made her film debut with Thodi Thodi Si Manmaaniyan opposite Arsh Sehrawat. In 2018, Parikh starred in Lamboo Rastoo opposite Jay Soni.
