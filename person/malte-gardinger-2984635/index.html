Malte Myrenberg Gårdinger (born July 23, 2000) is a Swedish actor. In 2021, he starred as August in the Netflix series Young Royals. He is the son of the Swedish television personality Pontus Gårdinger. Since 2021, Gårdinger released a couple of singles under the name Gibbon.
