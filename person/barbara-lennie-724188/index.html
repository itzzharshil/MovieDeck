Bárbara Lennie

  • Actress
  • 45 titles
  • Born 20 April 1984

Bárbara Lennie (20 April 1984) is a Spanish actress (of Argentine parents). She graduated in Interpretation from the Royal School of Dramatic Art (Madrid). She debuted at only fifteen in the 2001 film No Pain, No Gain and has won a Goya Award of best actress in 2015 for the film Magical Girl.

Madrid, Spain
