Vysakh is an Indian film director best known for his work in Malayalam commercial cinema. He made a sensational debut with Pokkiri Raja (2010) and went on to deliver major blockbusters like Pulimurugan (2016) and Madhura Raja (2019), earning acclaim for his mass entertainers and large scale action dramas.
