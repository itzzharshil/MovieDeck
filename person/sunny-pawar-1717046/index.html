Sunny Pawar

  • 5 titles

Sunny Pawar (born 2008) is an Indian actor, best known for portraying the young Saroo Brierley in the Australian biographical drama Lion (2016). His critically acclaimed performance earned him 10 award nominations and 3 wins, including the AACTA Award for Best Lead Actor. He also appeared in the thriller series Sacred Games (2018-2019) and the Italian film The Tiger's Nest (2023).
