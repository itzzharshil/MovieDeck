Edvin Ryding

Lars Edvin Folke Ryding (known as Edvin Ryding) is a Swedish actor. His debut was in the TV series "Mannen under trappan" of 2009. Ryding has since acted in several other productions "Fröken Frimans krig", "Kronjuvelerna", "The Stig-Helmer Story", "Gåsmamman", and various films about Annika Bengtzon, which were produced in 2011. He was discovered internationally for starring in the 2021 Netflix series "Young Royals", where he was afforded the role of Prince Wilhelm. He has also played the lead for the voice-role in the Danish children's animated film "Resan till Fjäderkungens rike".
