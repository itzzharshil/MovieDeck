Hokuto Matsumura

  • Actor
  • 64 titles
  • Born 18 June 1995

Hokuto Matsumura (松村 北斗) is a member of Japanese idol group SixTONES (pronounced "Stones"). He started acting in 2012 NTV drama Shiritsu Bakaleya Koukou. In 2025, he won Best Male Actor in a Leading Role at the 98th Kinema Junpo Best Ten Awards for his role as Takatoshi Yamazoe in Yoake no Subete (English title: All the Long Nights).
