Melissa Gedeón Pérez (born June 19, 1996), better known as Meli G, is a Mexican voice actress and singer. She is best known for voicing Pinkie Pie in the Latin American Spanish version of My Little Pony: Friendship Is Magic, Lucy Pevensie in The Chronicles of Narnia film trilogy and Demencia in Villainous.
