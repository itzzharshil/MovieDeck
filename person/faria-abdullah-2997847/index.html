Faria Abdullah

  • Actress
  • 15 titles
  • Born 28 May 1998

Faria Abdullah (born May 28, 1998) is an Indian actress and dancer who works predominantly in Telugu cinema and Hindi streaming productions. After performing in theatre and training in various dance forms, she made her feature film debut in the comedy *Jathi Ratnalu* (2021), earning a nomination for the SIIMA Award for Best Female Debut – Telugu.

Following her debut, Abdullah appeared in *Most Eligible Bachelor* (2021), *Bangarraju* (2022), the comedy thriller *Like, Share & Subscribe* (2022), and the action thriller *Ravanasura* (2023). In 2023, she made her Hindi streaming debut in the investigative thriller series *The Jengaburu Curse*.

Her subsequent credits included *Aa Okkati Adakku* (2024), *Kalki 2898 AD* (2024), and the comedy thriller *Mathu Vadalara 2* (2024). She later appeared in *Gurram Paapi Reddy* (2025) and *Gaayapadda Simham* (2026).
