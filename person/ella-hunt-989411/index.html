Ella Hunt

Ella Hunt is an English actress, known for Intruders, Les Misérables, Robot Overlords, and Anna and the Apocalypse. From 2016 until 2018, she played the role of Ellie Marsden in the ITV comedy series Cold Feet. In 2017, she appeared as Emma Carr in the ITV drama series Endeavour. Wikipedia
