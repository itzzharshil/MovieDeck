Choo Young-woo

  • Actor
  • 11 titles
  • Born 5 June 1999

Choo Young-woo is a South Korean actor who has quickly established himself in the industry through a mix of high-school dramas, slice-of-life romance, and ambitious historical and medical series. He is celebrated for his tall stature, compelling gaze, and ability to embody roles ranging from intense rivals to heartwarming protagonists.

His officially debuted in 2021 with the web drama You Make Me Dance, followed by a prominent supporting role in the KBS drama Police University (2021) as the stoic and competitive student, Park Min-kyu.
