Michiko Nomura

  • Actress
  • 54 titles
  • Born 31 March 1938

Michiko Nomura was born on March 31, 1938 in Kanagawa, Japan. She is an actress, known for Mahha GoGoGo (1967), Ecchan the Ninja (1971) and Serendipity Stories: Friends on Pure Island (1983). She was previously married to Kenji Utsumi.
