Sallie Harmsen

  • 46 titles

Sallie Harmsen (born May 2, 1989) is a Dutch actress having had roles in In Real Life for which she won Golden Calf for Best Actress at the Netherlands Film Festival 2008. Harmsen appeared in De Geheimen van Barslet Golden Calf for Best Actress in a TV drama in 2012. She has been recognised for her work in The Heineken Kidnapping (2011), Polleke, Vrijdag, and Tasso (2014-2015), Accused (Lucia de B.) (2014), and Moloch (2022). (wikipedia)
