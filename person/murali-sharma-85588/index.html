Murali Sharma

  • Actor
  • 185 titles
  • Born 9 August 1972

Murali Sharma is an Indian film character actor known for his works predominantly in Bollywood, Tollywood (Telugu), and Television. He is widely known for portraying a cop on screen and is considered one of the finest character artists of his generation across the country. Sharma has starred in feature films, including Telugu, Tamil, Marathi and Malayalam cinema.
