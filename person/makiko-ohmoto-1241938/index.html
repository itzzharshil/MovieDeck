Makiko Ohmoto (大本 眞基子, Ōmoto Makiko, born February 1, 1973) is a Japanese voice actress and narrator. She was born in Kurashiki City, Okayama Prefecture. Currently freelance, she was previously affiliated with Atomic Monkey.
