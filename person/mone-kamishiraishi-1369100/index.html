Mone Kamishiraishi

  • Actress
  • 58 titles
  • Born 27 January 1998

Mone Kamishiraishi is a Japanese actress, voice actress and singer affiliated with Toho Entertainment and Universal Music Japan. The older sister of actress Moka Kamishiraishi, she made her acting debut in theater and on television in 2011, following her win of the Special Jury Prize at the Toho Cinderella Audition. She was named a Newcomer of the Year at the 38th Japan Academy Prize for her role in the 2014 musical comedy film Maiko wa Lady.
