Jung Hae-in

  • Actor
  • 28 titles
  • Born 1 April 1988

Jung Hae-in is a South Korean actor. He first gained recognition with supporting roles in the television dramas While You Were Sleeping (2017) and Prison Playbook (2017–2018). He has since starred in Something in the Rain (2018), One Spring Night (2019), D.P. (2021–2023), Snowdrop (2021–2022), Love Next Door (2024) and Our Sticky Love (2026). Jung has also starred in the films Tune in for Love (2019), Start-Up (2019), and I, the Executioner (2024).
