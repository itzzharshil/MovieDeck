Russ Fega

  • Actor
  • 18 titles

Russ Fega is an American actor and location manager whose career spans from 1980 to the present. He is best known for appearing in five Christopher Nolan films: Memento (2000), The Prestige (2006), Inception (2010), The Dark Knight Rises (2012), and Interstellar (2014). He also served as location manager on Memento and The Prestige. His other film credits include Used Cars (1980), Mobsters (1991), and I Am Sam (2001). On television he has appeared in Babylon 5, Star Trek: Voyager, Murder, She Wrote, and Suddenly Susan. He has 21 location management credits including Body of Proof (16 episodes) and Curb Your Enthusiasm. His date and place of birth are unconfirmed. No major awards are on record.
