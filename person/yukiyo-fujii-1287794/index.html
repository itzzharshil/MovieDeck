Yukiyo Fujii (藤井 ゆきよ, Fujī Yukiyo, born May 8, 1985) is a Japanese actor and voice actress (seiyuu) born in Shimane Prefecture, and raised in Kanagawa Prefecture. She belongs to Aoni Production.
