Yūki Himura (日村 勇紀, Himura Yūki, born 14 May 1972, in Kurose, Higashihiroshima (formerly Kurose Kamo District, Hiroshima Prefecture) is a Japanese comedian, actor and voice actor. He performs tsukkomi (basically he did not decided if either he perform boke or tsukkomi, but can also perform boke) in the comedy duo Bananaman. His partner is Osamu Shitara. Himura married Aika Kanda, a Television announcer in April, 2018.
