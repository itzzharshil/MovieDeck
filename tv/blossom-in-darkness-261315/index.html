About Blossom in Darkness

Qi Fengyin, a feared demon lord blamed for all evil, encounters Ya Mao, a girl whose flesh is coveted for its extraordinary powers. Forced into a bond, the two clash yet gradually become each other's salvation. Together, they unravel six bizarre mysteries involving deadly curses, demons, resurrection, and forbidden love. As they expose a hidden conspiracy, they clear their names, free the world from its shackles, and give lovers a chance to stay together.
