Pradeep Ranganathan is an Indian filmmaker and actor known for his work in Tamil cinema. He made his directorial debut with the romantic comedy Comali (2019), which explored modern dependence on technology and was both a commercial and critical success. He later directed and starred in Love Today (2022), adapted from his own short film, where his performance and writing gained widespread acclaim for their humor and contemporary themes. Pradeep is recognized for his youthful storytelling style, blending romance, satire, and social commentary.
