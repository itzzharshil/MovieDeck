Matthew Maher (born 5 October 1971, Arkabutla, Mississippi) is an American film, television, and theatre actor (1.82 m). With deep roots in the New York theatre scene, he has performed at The Public Theater, New York Shakespeare Festival, Berkeley Rep, and Actors Theatre of Louisville. He studied the Meisner technique at the University of California, Santa Cruz. He is best known for voicing Wade Hebert in Grand Theft Auto V (2013) and for recurring roles in Our Flag Means Death (2022–2023, 18 episodes) and Outer Range (2022–2024, 6 episodes). His filmography includes: Dogma (1999) (Ben Affleck, Matt Damon); Bringing Out the Dead (1999) (Nicolas Cage, Tobey Maguire); Jersey Girl (2004) (Frida Pinto, Zac Efron); Gone Baby Gone (2007) (Casey Affleck, Michelle Monaghan); The Killer Inside Me (2010) (Essay Davis, Kate Mara); It's Kind of a Funny Story (2010) (Keira Knightley, Dane DeHaan); A Most Violent Year (2014) (Oscar Isaac, Jessica Chastain); While We're Young (2014) (Ben Stiller, Naomi Watts); The Finest Hours (2016) (Chris Pine, Casey Affleck); Live by Night (2016) (Ben Affleck, Sienna Miller); Lady Bird (2017) (Saoirse Ronan, Laurie Metcalf); Captain Marvel (2019) (Brie Larson, Annette Bening); Marriage Story (2019) (Adam Driver, Scarlett Johansson); Funny Pages (2022) (Jason Sudeikis, Lily Collins); Air (2023) (Matthew Royce, Ben Affleck); Relay (2024) (Riz Ahmed, Lily James); The Mastermind (2025) (Josh O'Connor, Ariana DeBose); Primetime (2026) (Sebastian Stan, Riz Ahmed); The Bride!
