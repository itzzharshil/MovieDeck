Kim Myung-min (born October 8, 1972) is a South Korean actor. He is best known for his leading roles in the television series Immortal Admiral Yi Sun-sin (2004), Beethoven Virus (2008), Six Flying Dragons (2015–2016), and Law School (2021), Your Honor (2024), as well as the films Closer to Heaven (2009) and the Detective K film series. Praised for his acting skills, he is often called "The Acting Expert".
