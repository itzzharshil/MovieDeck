Guy Burnet was born on August 8, in England. He is an actor, known for Ray Donovan (2017), Counterpart (2019) and The Affair (2018). He is also known for his theater work in London and New York, including rave reviews for 'Murder in the first'. He's also a proficient amateur boxer.
