Anthony Adam Ippolito (born August 21, 1999) is an American actor. He is known for his portrayals of Al Pacino in the Paramount+ miniseries The Offer (2022) and Sylvester Stallone in the biographical film I Play Rocky (2026). He also starred as George Wright in the Netflix drama series Grand Army (2020).
