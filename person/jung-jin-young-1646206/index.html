Jung Jin-young (born November 18, 1991), professionally known as Jinyoung, is a South Korean singer, songwriter, record producer, and actor. He was the leader and vocalist of the South Korean boy group B1A4 under WM Entertainment. He has gained recognition as an actor through Love in the Moonlight, Sweet Home, and Police University.
