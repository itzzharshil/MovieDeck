Liu Yuxuan

Liu Yuxuan (刘雨轩) is Chinese voice actor also known by the stage name Heishi Rěn (黑石稔). He formerly worked for the dubbing studio of Shanghai Yinxiong Culture Media Co., Ltd. and is a member of the Chinese dubbing club of the Sound Creation Alliance. His stage name Heishi Rěn cames from his idolization of Japanese voice actor Minoru Shiraishi, sometimes referred to as "Black Stone".
