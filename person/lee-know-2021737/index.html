Lee Know

Lee Min-ho, known professionally as Lee Know, is a South Korean singer and dancer under JYP Entertainment. He is a member of the boy group Stray Kids.

In addition to his work with Stray Kids, Lee Know hosted the music chart show Show! Music Core (2021-2023), and serves as a global ambassador for Gucci.
