This collection includes the new series of films from The Hunger Games franchise. Serving as both a prequel and an extension of the classic four‑film saga, the audience is invited deeper into the history of Panem and the origin stories of its key figures. The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (2023) follows the early life of Coriolanus Snow and the 10th Hunger Games. Sunrise on the Reaping (2026) centers on a young Haymitch Abernathy during the 50th Hunger Games, aka the Second Quarter Quell. Together, these works further expand the vast universe of The Hunger Games, revealing new truths about power, rebellion, and survival.
