Catherine Tsang

Catherine Tsang (曾勵珍), also known as Sister Tsang (珍姐), is a Hong Kong television director. She began her entertainment career in the early 1970s as a radio broadcaster for Hong Kong Radio Station. She joined TVB in 1974, starting as an assistant director, then working as a director and was later promoted to Deputy Director of the Drama Division.
