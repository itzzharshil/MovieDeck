Kazuhiko Inoue was born on March 26, 1954 in Yokohama, Japan. He is an actor, known for Naruto: Shippûden (2007), Naruto (2002) and Beast King GoLion (1981).

Murasakibana Kaoru is an alias Inoue Kazuhiko has used for adult games and anime.
