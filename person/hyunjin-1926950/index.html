HyunJin

Kim Hyun-jin (김현진), also known simply as HyunJin (현진), is the second revealed member of LOONA and a member of its first sub-unit, LOONA 1/3. She is a South Korean singer. In September 2023, she redebuted in Loossemble under the CTDENM label. She also became the group's captain/leader.
