Rodel Luis Pacheco Nacianceno (born 01 November 1981) is a Filipino actor, director, producer, and screenwriter. He is best known for his roles in the films The Masseur (2005), Service (2008), Jay (2008), Butchered (2009), You're My Boss (2015), and Beauty and the Bestie (2015). He was named Actor of the Decade at the 34th Gawad Urian Awards.
