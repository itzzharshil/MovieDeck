Motaz Malhees was born in 1992 in Jenin, Palestine. He began acting at the age of nine. He joined The Freedom Theatre Professional Acting School at 16. He was one of Juliano Mer Khamis' students, and he graduated three years later, joining an elite group of Palestinian professional actors.
