Jeremy Zag

  • Producer
  • 18 titles
  • Born 24 March 1985

Jeremy Zag (born August 24, 1985) is a French entrepreneur and award-winning producer, director, writer, and music composer for films, television series and digital entertainment for kids and families, and serves as the Founder, CEO & CCO of ZAG, a global independent studio. A self-described "kid at heart," Jeremy's upbringing in Paris exposed him to the worlds of art, fashion, and architecture, infusing him with an eye for design and a passion to create. Armed with a unique point of view and a gift for storytelling, Jeremy followed his dream to learn about the art of animation, beginning to create series and movies inspired by American heroes and Japanese superheroes.
