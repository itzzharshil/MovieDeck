Lollu Sabha Maaran is an Indian actor and comedian who has appeared in Tamil cinema. He became a well-known face when he begins to act in the mega-hit television show Lollu Shaba which aired on Vijay television. He started his career with the television show Lollu Sabha alongside other comedians, including Santhanam.
