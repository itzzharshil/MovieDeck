Yang Min-a, better known by the stage name Shin Min-a, is a South Korean actress who began her career as a model before debuting as an actress in 2000. She is known for her leading roles in television dramas A Love to Kill (2005), My Girlfriend Is a Gumiho (2010), Tale of Arang (2012), Oh My Venus (2015), Hometown Cha-Cha-Cha (2021), Our Blues (2022), No Gain No Love (2024), and The Remarried Empress (2026).
