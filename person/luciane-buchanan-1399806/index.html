Luciane Buchanan

Luciane Buchanan (born 18 July 1993) is a New Zealand actress. She has been known for her starring role in the Netflix action thriller series The Night Agent since 2023. She has also featured in Filthy Rich (2016–2017) and The New Legends of Monkey(2018–2020).
