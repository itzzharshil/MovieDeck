Daran Morrison Nordland (born November 1, 1964), known professionally as Daran Norris, is a European-American actor and voice actor. He is best known for voicing Cosmo, Mr. Turner, and Jorgen on Nickelodeon's The Fairly OddParents. He had recurring live-action television roles in Ned's Declassified School Survival Guide (2004 - 2007), Veronica Mars (2004 - 2019), Big Time Rush (2009 - 2013), and iZombie (2015 - 2019).
