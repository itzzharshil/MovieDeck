Natsuki Deguchi

  • Actress
  • 35 titles
  • Born 4 October 2001

Natsuki Deguchi is a Japanese model and actress. She made her acting debut on January 2019. Deguchi's mother is from China.

In 2018 She won Miss Seventeen 2018 along with 5 other contestants, and became an exclusive model for the fashion magazine Seventeen. She graduated from Seventeen on August 26, 2022.
