Victor Ma (simplified Chinese: 马伯骞; traditional Chinese: 馬伯騫) is a Chinese-American singer, songwriter, rapper, and actor under WAJIJIWA Entertainment. He attended University of Southern California. In 2018, he released his solo debut single, "I Am Awake". In 2021, he featured in the Chinese film, Detective Chinatown 3.
