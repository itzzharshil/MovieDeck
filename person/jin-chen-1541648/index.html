Jin Chen

Jin Chen (born 5 September 1990), also known as Gina Jin, is a Chinese actress and model of Hui descent. She is best known for Croczilla (2012), Song of the Assassins (2018), and S.W.A.T. (2019).

Jinan, Shandong Province, China
