Bagavathi Perumal

Bagavathi Perumal, popularly known as Bucks, is an Indian actor who has worked in Tamil films. After starting his career as an assistant director, Perumal debuted in the dark comedy film Naduvula Konjam Pakkatha Kaanom (2012), portraying himself in a story inspired by a real life event. The film received critical acclaim and Perumal garnered positive reviews for his performance. He has gone on to star in several films, mainly in supporting character roles. In 2019, he played a corrupt police officer in the film Super Deluxe, which garnered rave reviews from critics.
