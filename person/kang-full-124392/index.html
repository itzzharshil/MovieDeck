Kang Full (강풀), born Kang Do-young on December 7, 1974, in Seoul, South Korea, is a South Korean webtoon author and screenwriter. Recognized as a pioneer of the Korean webtoon industry, he began publishing digital comics in 2002 and developed a massive following for his intricate storytelling across romance, thriller, and supernatural genres.

Many of his acclaimed webtoons were adapted into high-profile Korean films, including APT. (2006), BA:BO (2008), Hello, Schoolgirl (2008), Late Blossom (2011), Pained (2011), The Neighbor (2012), and 26 Years (2012). Kang made his formal screenwriting debut with the global hit Disney+ original series Moving (2023), adapting his own superhero webtoon, which won the Grand Prize at the Baeksang Arts Awards. He subsequently penned the series adaptation for Light Shop (2024).
