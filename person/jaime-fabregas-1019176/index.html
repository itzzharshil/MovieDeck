Jaime Fabregas

  • Actor
  • 243 titles
  • Born 28 February 1950

Jaime Francisco García Fábregas is a Filipino actor and composer. Fábregas began his acting career in theatre before entering show business. He became a part of Repertory Philippines where he acted in many plays and musicals. He is a five-time Gawad Urian and two-time FAMAS winner.

Born in
Iriga City, Camarines Sur, Philippines
