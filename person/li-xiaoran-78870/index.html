Li Xiaoran

Li Xiaoran (Chinese: 李小冉) is a Chinese actress born on May 8, 1976. She graduated from Beijing Dance Academy at the age of 17, and was the only dancer recruited into the Oriental Song and Dance Troupe from Beijing.
