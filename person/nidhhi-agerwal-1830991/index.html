Nidhhi Agerwal (born 17 August 1993) is an Indian actress, model, and trained dancer who works predominantly in Telugu, Tamil, and Hindi cinema. She trained in classical and contemporary dance, including ballet, Kathak, and belly dance, and was a finalist in the Yamaha Fascino Miss Diva 2014 pageant before beginning her acting career.

She made her feature-film debut opposite Tiger Shroff in Sabbir Khan's Hindi dance action film *Munna Michael* (2017), for which she received the Zee Cine Award for Best Female Debut. She later appeared in the Telugu romantic and action films *Savyasachi* (2018) and *Mr. Majnu* (2019). She had a career breakthrough playing Dr. Sarah in Puri Jagannadh's science-fiction action film *iSmart Shankar* (2019), opposite Ram Pothineni.

Agerwal made her Tamil cinema debut in Lakshman's action drama *Bhoomi* (2021), followed by the rural comedy *Eeswaran* (2021). She later starred in Sriram Adittya's Telugu romantic comedy *Hero* (2022) and the Tamil action thriller *Kalaga Thalaivan* (2022), opposite Udhayanidhi Stalin. Her subsequent roles include Panchami in the period action film *Hari Hara Veera Mallu*, alongside Pawan Kalyan, and a leading role opposite Prabhas in Maruthi's horror-comedy *The Raja Saab*.
