Skyler Augustus Gisondo (born July 22, 1996) is an American actor. His first acting role was in a Pizza Hut commercial in 2002. He is known for his roles in the films Vacation (2015), The Social Dilemma (2020), Superman (2025), and Primetime (2026). He also had roles in TV series such as Psych (2010-2012), The Righteous Gemstones (2019-2025), and Santa Clarita Diet (2017-2019).
