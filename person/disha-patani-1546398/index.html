Disha Patani

  • 19 titles
  • Born 13 June 1992

Disha Patani (born June 13, 1992) is an Indian actress who works predominantly in Hindi cinema, alongside appearances in Telugu and Tamil films. She made her acting debut in the Telugu action drama *Loafer* (2015) before gaining mainstream recognition portraying Priyanka Jha in the biographical sports drama *M.S. Dhoni: The Untold Story* (2016), which earned her the IIFA Award for Star Debut of the Year – Female. She subsequently co-starred in the action-adventure comedy *Kung Fu Yoga* (2017), starred in the action thriller *Baaghi 2* (2018), and appeared in the period drama *Bharat* (2019).

In the early 2020s, Patani appeared in the romantic action thriller *Malang* (2020), *Radhe* (2021), and the psychological thriller *Ek Villain Returns* (2022). In 2024, she appeared in the action thriller *Yodha*, the Telugu science-fiction film *Kalki 2898-AD*, and the Tamil fantasy action feature *Kanguva*. Her later credits include *Welcome To The Jungle* (2026), *Awarapan 2* (2026).
