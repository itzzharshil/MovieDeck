Yuko Miyamura

  • Actress
  • 87 titles
  • Born 4 December 1972

Yuko Miyamura is a Japanese actress, voice actress, singer and sound director. Her nickname is Miyamū. She played Kazuha Toyama in Detective Conan and Asuka Langley Soryu in Neon Genesis Evangelion.

Born in
Kobe, Hyogo, Japan
