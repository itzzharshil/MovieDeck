Satomi Korogi

Satomi Korogi (こおろぎ さとみ, Kōrogi Satomi) is a Japanese voice actress affiliated with Production Baobab. She uses the aliases Jun Asano, Rie Nakagi, Madoka Misono and Haruka Kawai for her work on adult content.

Born in
Musashino, Tokyo, Japan
