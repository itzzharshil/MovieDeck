Kabir Duhan Singh

Kabir Duhan Singh is an Indian film actor, who has appeared in Telugu and Tamil language films. After making his debut in the Telugu film Jil (2015), Kabir has carved out a career as an antagonist in Tollywood. He acted in Sardar Gabbar Singh too.
