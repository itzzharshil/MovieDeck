Tomer Capone

  • Actor
  • 14 titles
  • Born 15 July 1985

Tomer Capone is an acclaimed Israeli film and television actor who achieved global recognition for his portrayal of Frenchie in the hit Amazon Prime Video satirical superhero series The Boys. Before breaking into international television, he established himself as a prominent talent in Israeli cinema and drama, earning critical acclaim and winning an Ophir Award for Best Supporting Actor for his role in the 2016 film One Week and a Day. On television, he gained widespread domestic and international attention starring in major Israeli hit series such as the espionage thriller Fauda and the prestigious drama When Heroes Fly, which won best series at the Canneseries Festival. Capone has successfully parlayed his intense dramatic training into a thriving cross-cultural career spanning high-profile streaming series and acclaimed feature films.
