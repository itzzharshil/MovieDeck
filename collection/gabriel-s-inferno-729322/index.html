A series of movies based off of the erotic romance novel Gabriel's Inferno written by an anonymous author under the pen name Sylvain Reynard. The original film was cut into three parts, each directed and produced by Tosca Musk and starring Melanie Zanetti and Giulio Berruti as Julia and Gabriel, respectively. The first three parts ("Gabriel's Inferno" I, II, and III) were released in 2020; the next two installments ("Gabriel's Rapture", Part One and Part Two) in 2021 and 2022.
