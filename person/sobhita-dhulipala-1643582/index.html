Sobhita Dhulipala

  • Actress
  • 18 titles
  • Born 31 May 1992

Sobhita Dhulipala (born 31 May 1992) is an Indian actress and model who works predominantly in Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil cinema, as well as in international productions. She won the *Femina Miss India Earth 2013* title and represented India at the Miss Earth 2013 pageant in the Philippines before beginning her acting career.

Dhulipala made her feature-film debut in Anurag Kashyap's neo-noir thriller *Raman Raghav 2.0* (2016), receiving a Best Supporting Performance nomination at the Cannes Film Festival's Directors' Fortnight. She later appeared in *Chef* (2017), the black comedy *Kaalakaandi* (2018), and her Telugu debut, the espionage thriller *Goodachari* (2018). She played Tara Khanna in the drama series *Made in Heaven* (2019–2023), and appeared in the spy thriller series *Bard of Blood* (2019), the horror anthology *Ghost Stories* (2020), the Malayalam crime drama *Kurup* (2021), and the biographical war drama *Major* (2022).

Dhulipala portrayed Vaanathi in Mani Ratnam's period films *Ponniyin Selvan: I* (2022) and *Ponniyin Selvan: II* (2023), and played Kaveri Dixit in the spy thriller series *The Night Manager* (2023). In 2024, she made her Hollywood debut in Dev Patel's action thriller *Monkey Man* and appeared in the family drama *Love, Sitara* (2024). She also starred in the dark comedy-drama *Cheekati Lo*.
