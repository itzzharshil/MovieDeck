Meenakshi Chaudhary

  • 17 titles

Meenakshi Chaudhary (born March 5, 1997) is an Indian actress, model, and beauty pageant titleholder who works primarily in Telugu and Tamil cinema. She was crowned Femina Miss India Grand International 2018 and finished as the first runner-up at Miss Grand International 2018.

Chaudhary made her acting debut in the web series Out of Love (2019) and later appeared as a lead in the Telugu film Ichata Vahanamulu Niluparadu (2021). She also acted in Khiladi (2022), HIT: The Second Case (2022), and made her Tamil film debut with Kolai (2023). Her subsequent film credits include Guntur Kaaram (2024), The Greatest of All Time (2024), Lucky Baskhar (2024), and Sankranthiki Vasthunam (2025).
