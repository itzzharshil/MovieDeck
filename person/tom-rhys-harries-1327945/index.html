Tom Rhys Harries

  • 26 titles

Tom Rhys Harries (born 8 October 1990) is a Welsh actor best known for his roles as Manchester DJ Axel Collins on White Lines (2020, Netflix), Eddie Walker on Suspicion (2022, Apple TV+), and, more recently, Matt Hagen in the DCU film Clayface (2026). He also had recurring roles on Jekyll and Hyde (2015), Unforgotten (2018), and Britannia (2019). In films, Harries has appeared in Hunky Dory (2011), Slaughterhouse Rulez (2018), The Gentlemen (2019), Sisi & I (2023), Kandahar (2023), and How Kids Roll (2024).
