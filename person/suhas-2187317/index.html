Suhas Pagolu is an Indian actor who shines in Telugu films. He started small but gained fame through short films and supporting roles. In 2020, he took the lead in "Colour Photo," which became a hit. Since then, he's been impressing us in movies like "Gamanam" and "Family Drama." Outside of acting, he tied the knot with Lalitha in August 2017.
