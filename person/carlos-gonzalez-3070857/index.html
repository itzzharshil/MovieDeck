Carlos González Fernández (born 24 May 1997) is a Spanish actor. Gonzalez studied acting at Estudio Corazza para la Actuación, the same school as fellow countryman Javier Bardem. Gonzalez had been acting in plays and shows, but hadn't made a debut in films until La Bola Negra (2026), playing Alberto. Gonzalez identifies as non-binary, and prefers both feminine and masculine pronouns.
