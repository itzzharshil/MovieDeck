Hao Xianghai

  • Actor
  • 36 titles
  • Born 28 July 1986

Hao Xianghai (郝祥海), also known as Slayerboom, is a Chinese voice actor and casting director. Hao Xianghai has voiced many characters from various bilibili series which include Fog Hill of Five Elements, White Cat Legend, and the Manual of Hundred Demon. Other roles include: Lynn Angel in All Saint's Street and Yuan Ding in Planting Manual, Fengxi from The Legend of Hei prequel movie, and the Demonic King Who Chases His Wife, Liuyun Nangong.
