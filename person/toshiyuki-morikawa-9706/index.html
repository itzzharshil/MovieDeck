Toshiyuki Morikawa

  • Actor
  • 382 titles
  • Born 26 January 1967

Toshiyuki Morikawa is a Japanese voice actor. He was formerly affiliated with Arts Vision until he co-founded his own talent agency Axl One in 2011.

Because of his deep voice, he is often cast as imposing characters.

Born in
Shinagawa, Tokyo, Japan
