Yasmin Finney

  • Actress
  • 11 titles
  • Born 30 August 2003

Yasmin Finney is an English actress and internet personality. She is known for her role as Elle Argent in the Netflix series Heartstopper, for which she was nominated for a Children's and Family Emmy Award for Outstanding Supporting Performance. She also plays Rose Noble in the BBC series Doctor Who.
