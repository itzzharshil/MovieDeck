Mariya Ise

  • Actress
  • 169 titles

Mariya Ise is a Japanese actress, voice actress and narrator from Kanagawa. She is affiliated with Across Entertainment. She is popularly known as the voice actress of Killua Zoldyck in Hunter × Hunter (2011), Reg in Made in Abyss, Ray in The Promised Neverland and Levy McGarden as well as Romeo in Fairy Tail.
