Curry Barker

Curry Barker is an American filmmaker, writer, actor, comedian, and YouTube creator. After building an audience with the sketch comedy channel that's a bad idea, which he co-created with Cooper Tomlinson, Barker broke out with the found footage horror film Milk & Serial (2024). His blockbuster sophomore feature, Obsession (2025), established him as one of horror's most exciting emerging filmmakers. He is next set to write and direct the supernatural horror film Anything but Ghosts.
