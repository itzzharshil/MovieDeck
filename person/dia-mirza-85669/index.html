Dia Mirza

  • Actress
  • 55 titles

Dia Mirza is an Indian model, actress, producer and beauty queen who won Miss Asia Pacific 2000 title. Mirza has primarily worked in Bollywood and is known in the media for her social work. She made her acting debut with Rehna Hai Tere Dil Mein.
