Hailey Benton Gates (born March 28, 1990) is an American model, actress and director. She is best known for her supporting roles in Marty Supreme (2025) and The Drama (2026). Her maternal grandmother is screenwriter Joan Tewkesbury, most known for writing the film Nashville (1975).
