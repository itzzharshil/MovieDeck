Arun Govil (born 12 January 1958) is an Indian actor and politician. He is best known for portraying Lord Rama in the 1987 Ramayan TV series and reprising his role (voice acting) in the 1993 animated film, Ramayana: The Legend of Prince Rama. He also appeared in films like Paheli (1977), Sawan Ko Aane Do (1979), Saanch Ko Aanch Nahin (1979), Jiyo To Aise Jiyo (1981), Himmatwala (1983), Dilwaala (1986), and Govinda Govinda (1994).
