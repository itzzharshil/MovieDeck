Larry Yang Zi is a Chinese director and screenwriter. He started his career as an actor in a short horror film "Dimension 13". Yang represents the new generation of bi-cultural talent in Chinese film, having honed his craft by earning Master's degrees in Film from both the Beijing Film Academy and the University of Edinburgh.
