Satoshi Inomata (猪股 慧士 Inomata Satoshi) is a Japanese voice actor affiliated with I'm Enterprise. He is known for voice acting in Love After World Domination (2022), Let This Grieving Soul Retire! (2024), The Ramparts of Ice (2026) and Sword Art Online (2012).
