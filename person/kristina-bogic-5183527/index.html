Kristina Bogic

Kristina Bogic, is an Australian actress and singer. She began her artistic career in music, winning the Wyndham Rising Stars competition at age 12 and releasing her debut single "Learn My Limits" in 2020. In acting, she gained recognition in her home country with the miniseries Playing Gracie Darling (2025). Her major breakthrough to international fame came in 2026 when she starred alongside Sara Boustany in the Netflix thriller series "The Body" (created by Quinn Shephard), playing Anya Butler, the captain of a Catholic school dance team. For this role, which marked her first time using an American accent, she underwent an intensive eight-week dance training boot camp.
