Chang Chen (Chinese: 張震; born October 14, 1976) is a Taiwanese actor, born in Taipei, Taiwan. His name is sometimes seen in the Western order (Chen Chang). He is the son of Taiwanese actor Chang Kuo Chu and brother of Taiwanese actor Chang Han.
