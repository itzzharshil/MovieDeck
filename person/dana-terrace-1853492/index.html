Dana Terrace

  • Filmmaker
  • 10 titles
  • Born 8 December 1990

Dana Terrace is a writer, director, and cartoonist. She is best known as the creator of the animated series The Owl House. Her previous work at Disney Television Animation included story boarding on Gravity Falls and working as a director on Ducktales. Dana is also the co-creator of the Glitch Productions animated web series Knights of Guinevere.
