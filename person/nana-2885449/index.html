Nana

  • Actress
  • 8 titles
  • Born 9 March 2001

Kwon Na-yeon, known professionally as Nana, is a South Korean singer, actress and dancer under H Music Entertainment. She is the leader of the girl group WOOAH and a former member of the project girl group EL7Z UP, having finished second on Mnet's survival reality show Queendom Puzzle (2023).

She is also a member of the fictional girl group IRION under the stage name Aji.
