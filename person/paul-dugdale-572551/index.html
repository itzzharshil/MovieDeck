Paul Dugdale

  • Director
  • 88 titles

Paul Dugdale is a British film director known for his acclaimed concert films and music documentaries made for major artists like Adele, Coldplay, and Taylor Swift. He studied at Central Saint Martins School of Art in London and has won multiple awards, including Emmys, BAFTAs, and DGAs for his work.
