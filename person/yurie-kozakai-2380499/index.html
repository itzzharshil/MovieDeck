Yurie Kozakai (小坂井 祐莉絵, Kozakai Yurie, born July 6, 1995) is a voice actress born in Higashiura City, Chita District, Aichi Prefecture. Her blood group is O. She is affiliated with Crocodile. She is a member of the voice actor unit GuilDrops.
