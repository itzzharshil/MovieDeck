Amber Doig-Thorne is a British Actress and Producer. Amber has played leading roles in over 25 Feature Films, numerous award winning short films, TV spots and comedy productions. She is also a Comedian, Director and Presenter. Amber has Irish and Scottish heritage; she is Neurodivergent (ADHD) and previously lived in New Zealand.
