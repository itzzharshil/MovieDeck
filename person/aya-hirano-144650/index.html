Aya Hirano

  • Actress
  • 121 titles
  • Born 8 October 1987

Aya Hirano is a Japanese voice actress and singer. After graduating from high school, she began seriously pursuing her voice actress and solo singing career. Her big break came in 2006, when she was cast as the voice of Suzumiya Haruhi, the title character of The Melancholy of Haruhi Suzumiya.

Born in
Nagoya, Aichi, Japan
