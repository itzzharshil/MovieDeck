Steven Bauer (born Esteban Ernesto Echevarría Samson; December 2, 1956) is a Cuban American businessman, entrepreneur, actor, producer and musician. He is known for his role as Manny Ribera in the 1983 film Scarface, and his role as Don Eladio on the crime drama show Breaking Bad (2011) and its spin-off Better Call Saul (2017–2022).
