Gino Saltamerenda

Gino Saltamerenda was born on June 2, 1902 in Rome, Lazio, Italy as Luigi Saltamerenda. He was an actor, known for Bicycle Thieves (1948), The Affairs of Messalina (1951) and Campane a martello (1949). He died on September 25, 1951 in Rome.
