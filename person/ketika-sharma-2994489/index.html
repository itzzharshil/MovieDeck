Ketika Sharma

  • 9 titles

Ketika Sharma (born 24 December 1995) is an Indian actress, model, and singer who works predominantly in Telugu cinema, as well as in Hindi and Tamil films. She began her career as a model and appeared in digital video content.

She made her feature-film debut opposite Akash Puri in Puri Jagannadh's romantic drama *Romantic* (2021). She later appeared in the sports drama *Lakshya* (2021), opposite Naga Shaurya, and Gireesaaya's romantic family drama *Ranga Ranga Vaibhavanga* (2022), alongside Panja Vaisshnav Tej. In Samuthirakani's fantasy comedy *Bro* (2023), she played Ramya alongside Pawan Kalyan and Sai Dharam Tej.

Sharma made her Hindi cinema debut in the sports drama *Vijay 69* (2024), starring Anupam Kher. She played Purva Nettem in the romantic comedy *#Single* (2025), opposite Sree Vishnu, and appeared in the song "Adhi Dha Surprisu" in Venky Kudumula's action film *Robinhood* (2025).
