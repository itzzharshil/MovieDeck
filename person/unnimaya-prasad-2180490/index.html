Unnimaya Prasad

  • Actress
  • 23 titles

Unnimaya Prasad is an Indian actress, assistant director and producer who works in Malayalam cinema. She worked as the casting director of Maheshinte Prathikaram, and as assistant director of Thondimuthalum Driksakshiyum. She is associated with the new wave of Malayalam cinema. She is also a practicing architect.

Born in
Kochi, Kerala, India
