Ramachandran Durairaj

  • Actor
  • 47 titles

Ramachandran Durairaj is an Indian film actor who has appeared in Tamil language films. After working as an associate director, he made his breakthrough as an actor with a leading role in Suseenthiran's Naan Mahaan Alla (2010). He later won acclaim for playing a leading role in Gopi Nainar's Aramm (2017).
