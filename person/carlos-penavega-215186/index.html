Carlos Roberto PenaVega (né Pena Jr.; born August 15, 1989) is a Native-Latino American actor and singer. He starred on the Nickelodeon series Big Time Rush as Carlos García, and is a member of the group Big Time Rush. He was also the host of the network's game show Webheads. He currently voices Bobby Santiago in The Loud House franchise.
