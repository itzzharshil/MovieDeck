Amanda Leighton

  • Actress
  • 18 titles
  • Born 7 June 1993

Amanda Moreno Leighton is an American actress. She is known for her voice acting as Blossom in the 2016 Cartoon Network animated series The Powerpuff Girls, Poppy in the 2018 Netflix animated series Trolls: The Beat Goes On!, and Polly Plantar in the 2019 Disney Channel animated series Amphibia.
