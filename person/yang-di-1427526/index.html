Yang Di

Yang Di, born in Wenchuan, Ngawa Tibetan and Qiang Autonomous Prefecture, is an actor and presenter in Mainland China. He graduated from Sichuan Normal University, School of Film and Television. In 2013, he was known for his role in Stephen Chow's movie "Journey to the West: Conquering the Demons" as San Sha. He is also a celebrated comedian, often referred to as the 'King of Variety Shows' for his remarkable contributions to the genre.
