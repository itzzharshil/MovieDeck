The Ne Zha movie collection includes two major films: Ne Zha (2019) and Ne Zha 2 (2025). Ne Zha (2019) is a Chinese animated adventure fantasy action film directed by Jiaozi, based on the Chinese mythological character Ne Zha and the 16th-century novel Investiture of the Gods. It received critical acclaim for its stunning animation and dynamic action. The film follows Ne Zha's journey as he faces a curse that will kill him in three years and his efforts to change his fate.
