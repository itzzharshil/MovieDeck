Shin Hae-sun

  • Actress
  • 29 titles
  • Born 31 August 1989

Shin Hae-sun is a South Korean actress. She debuted in the television series School 2013. In 2017, Shin played her first leading role and became well known in the weekend drama, My Golden Life, which garnered more than 8 million viewers. She is also best known for her other leading roles in Korean dramas such as Still 17 (2018), Angel's Last Mission: Love (2019), Stranger (2017–2020), Mr. Queen (2020–2021), See You in My 19th Life (2023), Welcome to Samdal-ri (2023–2024), The Art of Sarah (2026), and Filing for Love (2026).
