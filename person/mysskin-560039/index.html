Mysskin

  • Director
  • 36 titles
  • Born 20 September 1971

Mysskin is a South Indian film director, screenwriter, actor, singer and producer. He made his directorial debut in 2006 with Chithiram Pesuthadi. His other directorials Anjathe, Nandalala and Onaayum Aattukkuttiyum received high critical acclaim. He has also proved his acting skills by starring as a mentally challenged person in Nandalala, as a killer seeking redemption in Onaiyum Aatukuttiyum, and as a superstitious cult leader in Super Deluxe.
