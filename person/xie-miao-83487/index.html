Xie Miao

  • Actor
  • 70 titles
  • Born 2 May 1984

Xie Miao is a Chinese actor and martial artist who began his career as a child actor. Born in Beijing, he won championship titles in several wushu tournaments. He rose to fame when he starred as a father-son duo with Jet Li in the films The New Legend of Shaolin and My Father Is a Hero.
