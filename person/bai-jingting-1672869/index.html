Bai Jingting

  • Actor
  • 25 titles
  • Born 15 October 1993

Bai Jing Ting is a Chinese actor born in Beijing, China. He studied at Capital Normal University's Music College, majoring in recording. He can play the piano and achieved a grade of ten in the non-professional players' level assessment.

Jing Ting began his acting career during the academic year of 2014. He made his debut in the web series "Back in Time". He is known for the series Ordinary Glory, You Are My Hero, Reset, New Life Begins, Destined, Always On The Move and The First Frost. He is the founder of 'GoodBai' merchandise and café, and artist management 'Bai Jingting Studio'.
