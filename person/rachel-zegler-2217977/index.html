Rachel Zegler

Rachel Anne Zegler (born May 3, 2001) is an American actress and singer. She made her film debut as María in Steven Spielberg’s musical adaptation West Side Story (2021). She later starred as Anthea in Shazam! Fury of the Gods (2023), Lucy Gray Baird in The Hunger Games: The Ballad of Songbirds & Snakes (2023), and the titular character in Snow White (2025). In 2024, she made her Broadway theatre debut as Juliet in a revival of Romeo + Juliet.
