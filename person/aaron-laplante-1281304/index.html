Aaron LaPlante

  • Actor
  • 17 titles
  • Born 9 March 1983

Aaron LaPlante is an American voice actor, writer, and mixed-media artist known for his deep, powerful vocal range and physically expressive creature work. He initially built his background performing with award-winning sketch comedy teams in Los Angeles. Over the years, his knack for creating highly distinct, unique voices for onstage characters transitioned into a prolific career across major animation, anime dubbing, and video game franchises.
