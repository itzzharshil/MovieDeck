Rogério Cardoso

Rogério Cardoso was a Brazilian actor, comedian, musician, composer and councilman. He established himself in several areas of entertainment, in a career spanning five decades, but it was in the comedy industry that he gained prominence, having performed in several comedy programs on the country's main broadcasters.
