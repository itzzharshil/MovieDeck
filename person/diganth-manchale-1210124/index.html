**Diganth Manchale** (born 28 December 1983), mononymously and popularly known as **Diganth**, is an acclaimed Indian actor and former model who primarily works in Kannada cinema. Affectionately referred to as the "Doodh Peda" of Sandalwood by his fans and the media, he is known for his charming screen presence, natural acting style, and successful collaborations with prominent directors.

Born in Sagara, Karnataka, and raised in Thirthahalli, Diganth completed his pre-university education in his hometown before moving to Bengaluru to earn a Bachelor of Commerce degree from Sri Bhagawan Mahaveer Jain College. Before transitioning to cinema, he worked as a model and appeared in various television commercials.

He made his lead acting debut in 2006 with the Kannada film *Miss California*. However, he first gained significant attention for his supporting role in the monumental 2006 blockbuster *Mungaru Male*. His major cinematic breakthrough arrived in 2008 with Yogaraj Bhat's massively successful romantic comedy *Gaalipata*. His charming performance in the film earned him widespread popularity and firmly established his enduring nickname.

Following this success, he proved his capabilities as a leading man in the industry with the critically acclaimed 2009 romantic drama *Manasaare*, where he played a solo hero to great acclaim. Throughout the 2010s and 2020s, he delivered memorable performances in a string of successful and beloved films, including *Pancharangi* (2010), *Lifeu Ishtene* (2011), *Parijatha* (2012), *Katheyondu Shuruvagide* (2018), *Gaalipata 2* (2022), and *Laughing Buddha* (2024). He also expanded his horizons by making his Bollywood debut in the 2015 film *Wedding Pullav* and taking on roles in Telugu cinema.
